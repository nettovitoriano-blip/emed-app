2  Procedure

2.1  Acquiring the 20cm QA Phantom Image Series

  1. Mount the Phantom Holder on the head-end of the table.

  1. Mount the 20cm QA Phantom on the Phantom Holder.

  1. Align, level, and center the 20cm QA Phantom.

    • Align the etched line (QA#1 position) on phantom using the internal laser lights.

    • Level phantom using bubble level and the Z Axis knob on the Phantom Holder.

    • Center phantom using the [CENTER PHANTOM] procedure in the left head [SCANNER UTILITIES ]selection and the X and Y Axis knobs on the Phantom Holder.

  1. Set up the system to scan the QA#1, QA#2, and QA#3 positions on the 20cm QA Phantom.

    MANUAL SCAN PROTOCOL SETUP

    Refer toTable 1 and setup an Axial scan with the parameters shown

    AUTO SCAN PROTOCOL SETUP

    1. On the Exam Rx desktop, select [NEW PATIENT].

    2. Type the following entries in the listed Patient Information following fields:

      • Patient ID:Service

      • Name:20cm QA Phantom Image Series

    3. From the Protocol Selection display, click on the Service Tab under Anatomical Selector.

    4. On the Service Protocol Selection window, click on [MANUFACTURING].

    5. On the Service Category Protocol List, for 4/8 slice system click on the [45.12 IMAGE SERIES QA] selection; for 16 slice system click on the [45.9 IMAGE SERIES 20CM QA] selection.

    6. On the ExamRx protocol parameter display, select the 1st Series (Series Description: QA1 MTF 2x10/120kV/260mA/1s).

  1. Set internal Landmark.

  1. Acquire four 20cm QA Phantom image series as following:

    1. Acquire the 1st 20cm QA Phantom image series and 2nd Recon series (MTF and Contrast, Visible Lines) by performing the 1st series protocol scan, the series name as “QA1 MTF 2x10/120kV/260mA/1s”. The 2nd Recon series is generated from QA1 MTF 2x10/120kV/260mA/1s automatically.

    1. For BrightSpeed Select Series and Optima CT520 Series: acquire the 3rd 20cm QA Phantom image series by performing the 3rd series protocol scan, the series name as “QA2 Ho 2x10/120kV/260mA/1s”. ; For BrightSpeed Elite/Optima CT540: acquire the 3rd 20cm QA Phantom image series by performing the 4th series protocol scan, the series name as “QA2 Ho 2x10/120kV/260mA/1s”.

    1. For BrightSpeed Select Series and Optima CT520 Series: acquire the 4th 20cm QA Phantom image series by performing the 4th series protocol scan, the series name as “QA3 Sm 2x10/120kV/260mA/1s”;For BrightSpeed Elite/Optima CT540: acquire the 4th 20cm QA Phantom image series by performing the 5th series protocol scan, the series name as “QA3 Sm 2x10/120kV/260mA/1s”.

2.2  High Contrast Spatial Resolution

  1. Specifications:

    Each image of the series must pass 20cm QA#1 High Contrast Spatial Resolution parameter (Contrast Scale, Four-Image MTF Average and Visible Line Pair (for the first and second series scan parameters) specifications:

    Contrast Scale110.0 to 130.0
    MTF Average:0.65 to 1.0
    Visible Lines:B, C, D, E, F

  1. Recommended Failure Recovery:

    1. Check Phantom Alignment (leveling is critical) and repeat this scanning and High Contrast Spatial Resolution Performance Test.

    1. Perform Alignment Procedures (POR Alignment, BOW Alignment, CBF/SAG Alignment, ISO Alignment, and Hot ISO Alignment), perform Full Calibration (Detailed Calibration and Auto CT# Adjust, and repeat this scanning and High Contrast Spatial Resolution Performance Test.

2.3  Low Contrast Detectability

2.3.1  Image Performance Verification

  1. Select the third 20cm QA Phantom image series (QA#2 Holes) from the exam acquired in the previous section.

    1. From the Global Control Palette, click on the [IMAGE WORKS] Desktop.

    1. From the Image Works Desktop, select the [IMAGE WORKS BROWSER] window.

    1. Select the exam and the third series acquired in the previous section.

    1. Select the [VIEWER] button on the Image Works Browser window. Set up the viewer window for four-image viewing.

  1. Build a 10 mm x 100 mm reference ROI Box using the Image Works Viewer tools.

    1. Click on the grid button to place a grid on the first image.

    1. Click on the [MEASURE] button and select the box ROI icon.

    1. Adjust the size of the ROI box to be a rectangle of approximately 10 mm x 50 mm (500 mm2) box.

      If required, magnify the image to adjust to proper dimensions. SeeIllustration 2 for additional ROI size and placement information.

  1. Collect Mean values for two reference ROI Box positions on four (1st, 3rd, 5th, and 7th) of the eight 20cm QA#2 Phantom images.

    If required, magnify the image to adjust to proper dimensions. SeeIllustration 2 for additional ROI placement information.

    1. Adjust the Window setting of the image to [20] by simultaneously holding the center mouse button down while dragging the cursor to the left. Adjust level for a viewable image as shown in Illustration 2.

    2. Position the reference ROI Box built in step 2 on the Plexiglas portion of the QA#2 phantom image without touching the water portion or the hole pattern. See Illustration 2.

    3. Type prop a in the Image Works Accelerator Line followed by <Return> to propagate Box # 1 ROI (size and position) on the remaining images in the series.

    4. Click on the [MEASURE] button and select the box ROI icon to display Box # 2. The system places an ROI box labeled # 2 at the center of the image with the exact same dimensions as Box # 1.

    5. Reposition Box # 2 over the water portion of the QA#1 phantom image and directly above Box # 1 (Box # 2 position in Illustration 2).

    6. Type prop a in the Image Works Accelerator Line followed by <Return> to propagate Box # 2 ROI (size and position) on the remaining images.

    7. Record the Mean values of the 1st, 3rd, 5th, and 7th images in the series for each of the two box positions in Table 2.

  1. View the 1st, 3rd, 5th, and 7th images of the QA#2 Holes Series, record the number of visible holes in Table 2, and verify each image meets specifications.

    1. While viewing the 1st, 3rd, 5th, and 7th images, indicate inTable 2 the number of holes that can be visually distinguished. See Illustration 2.

    1. Verify Visible Hole visual check meets specifications listed inTable 3 for the calculated Contrast Factor and record in the HHS Record Tables.

2.3.2  Failure Recovery

  1. Specifications:

    At least two out of the four images of the series must pass the 20cm QA#2 Low Contrast Detectability parameter (Visible Hole Size for a calculated Contrast Factor) for the third series scan parameters specifications.

  1. Recommended Recovery:

    1. Check Phantom Alignment (leveling is critical) and repeat this scanning and High Contrast Spatial Resolution Performance Test.

    1. Perform Alignment Procedures (POR Alignment, BOW Alignment, CBF/SAG Alignment, ISO Alignment, and Hot ISO Alignment), perform Full Calibration (Detailed Calibration and Auto CT# Adjust, and repeat this scanning and Low Contrast Detectability Performance Test.

2.4  QA#3 Phantom Brightness Uniformity and CT#

2.4.1  Performance Verification

  1. Select the fourth 20cm QA#3 Phantom exam acquired in the previous section.

    1. From the Global Control Palette, click on the [IMAGE WORKS] Desktop.

    1. From the Image Works Desktop, select the [IMAGE WORKS BROWSER] window.

    1. Select the exam and fourth (QA#3) series acquired in the previous section.

    1. Select the [VIEWER] button on the Image Works Browser window. Set up the viewer window for four-image viewing.

  1. Build a 31 x 31 pixel reference ROI Box using the Image Works Viewer tools.

    1. Click on the grid button to place a grid on the first image.

    1. Click on the [MEASURE] button and select the box ROI icon.

    1. Adjust the size of the ROI box to be a square 15 mm x 15 mm (225 mm2) box. Tolerance: 15 mm ± 1 mm (196 mm2 to 256 mm2).

      If required, magnify the image to adjust to proper dimensions. SeeIllustration 3 for additional ROI size and placement information.

  1. Collect Mean values for five reference ROI Box positions on the eight 20cm QA#3 Phantom images.

    If required, magnify the image to adjust to proper dimensions. SeeIllustration 3 for additional ROI placement information.

    1. Position the reference ROI Box built in step 2 directly over the center of the image using the grid cross-hairs as a guide.

    2. Type prop a in the Image Works Accelerator Line followed by <Return> to propagate Box # 1 ROI (size and position) on the remaining images in the series.

    3. Click on the [MEASURE] button and select the box ROI icon to display Box # 2. The system places an ROI box labeled # 2 at the center of the image with the exact same dimensions as Box # 1.

    4. Reposition Box # 2 to the left center portion on the first image.

    5. Type prop a in the Image Works Accelerator Line followed by <Return> to propagate Box # 2 ROI (size and position) on the remaining images.

    6. Record the Mean values of the eight images in the series for each of the five box positions in Table 4.

      Each image can only display text for the mean, standard deviation, and box area for three images at a time. To view the data for a particular box, select the box on the image and the system displays the data for the box number selected.

  1. Calculate the Brightness Uniformity and CT# values for each image in the series, record values in Table 5, and compare to specifications.

    1. Calculate and record the average means values (AvXo) for the four outside Boxes (Boxes 2 through 5) for each of the images and record in Table 5.

    1. Calculate and record the average center box (Box 1) means values (AvXc) for each row (2A1A: Images 1, 3, 5, and 7; 1B2B: Images 2, 4, 6, 8) in Table 5.

    1. Calculate and record the average outside boxes (Box 2 through 5) means values (AvXo) for each row (2A1A: Images 1, 3, 5, and 7; 1B2B: Images 2, 4, 6, 8) in Table 5.

    1. Calculate the Brightness Uniformity (AvXo - AvXc) value for each row and record in Table 5.

    1. Verify Brightness Uniformity (AvXo - AvXc) value and the average CT# value (AvXc) for each row meets specifications listed in Table 5.

    1. Record the 20cm QA#3 Phantom Brightness Uniformity (AvXo - AvXc) value and average CT# value (AvXc) for each row in the HHS Record Tables.

2.4.2  Failure Recovery

  1. Specifications:

    Each Row (2A1A, 1B2B) of the series must pass 20cm QA#3 Phantom Brightness Uniformity and average CT# specifications:

    AvXo - AvXc:< ± 3.0
    AvXc:< ± 3.0

  1. Recommended Recovery:

    1. Perform [DETAILED CAL].

    1. Perform [AUTO CT# ADJUST].

    1. Repeat this procedure to verify 20cm QA#3 Phantom Image Performance.

2.5  QA#3 Phantom Noise

2.5.1  Performance Verification

  1. Select the fourth 20cm QA#3 Phantom exam acquired in the previous section.

    1. From the Global Control Palette, click on the [IMAGE WORKS] Desktop.

    1. From the Image Works Desktop, select the [IMAGE WORKS BROWSER] window.

    1. Select the exam and fourth (QA#3) series acquired in the previous section.

    1. Select the [VIEWER] button on the Image Works Browser window. Set up the viewer window for four-image viewing.

  1. Build a 51 x 51 pixel reference ROI Box using the Image Works Viewer tools.

    1. Click on the grid button to place a grid on the first image.

    1. Click on the [MEASURE] button and select the box ROI icon.

    1. Adjust the size of the ROI box to be a square 25 mm x 25 mm (625 mm2) box. Tolerance: 25 mm ± 1 mm (576 mm2 to 676 mm2).

      If required, magnify the image to adjust to proper dimensions. SeeIllustration 4 for additional ROI size and placement information.

  1. Collect Standard Deviation values for the single reference ROI Box position on the eight 20cm QA#3 Phantom images.

    If required, magnify the image to adjust to proper dimensions. SeeIllustration 4 for additional ROI placement information.

    1. Position the reference ROI Box built in step 2 directly over the center of the image using the grid cross-hairs as a guide.

    2. Type prop a in the Image Works Accelerator Line followed by <Return> to propagate Box # 1 ROI (size and position) on the remaining images in the series.

    3. Record the Standard Deviation values of the eight images in the series for centered box position in Table 6.

  1. Calculate the average Noise values for each image in the series, record values in Table 6, and compare to specifications.

    1. Calculate and record the average Noise values (AvSDc) for the inside Boxes for each of the two rows (2A1A and 1B2B) and record in Table 6.

    1. Verify Noise (AvSDc) values for each row meets specifications listed in Table 6.

    1. Record the 20cm QA#3 Phantom Noise (AvSDc) for each row in the HHS Record Tables.
