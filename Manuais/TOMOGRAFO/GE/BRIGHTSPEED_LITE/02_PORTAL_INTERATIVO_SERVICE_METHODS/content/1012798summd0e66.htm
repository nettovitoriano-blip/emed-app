AUTO SCAN PROTOCOL SETUP

  1. On the Exam Rx desktop, select [NEW PATIENT].

  2. Type the following entries in the listed Patient Information following fields:

    • Patient ID:Service

    • Name:20cm QA Phantom Image Series

  3. From the Protocol Selection display, click on the Service Tab under Anatomical Selector.

  4. On the Service Protocol Selection window, click on [MANUFACTURING].

  5. On the Service Category Protocol List, for 4/8 slice system click on the [45.12 IMAGE SERIES QA] selection; for 16 slice system click on the [45.9 IMAGE SERIES 20CM QA] selection.

  6. On the ExamRx protocol parameter display, select the 1st Series (Series Description: QA1 MTF 2x10/120kV/260mA/1s).
