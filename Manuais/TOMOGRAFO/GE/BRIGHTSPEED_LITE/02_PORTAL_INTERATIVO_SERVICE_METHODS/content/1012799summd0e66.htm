MANUAL SCAN PROTOCOL SETUP

Refer toTable 1 and setup an Axial scan with the parameters as shown.

AUTO SCAN PROTOCOL SETUP

  1. On the Exam Rx desktop, select [NEW PATIENT].

  2. Type the following entries in the two listed Patient Information fields:

    Patient ID: Service

    Name: 48cm Phantom Image Series

  3. From the Protocol Selection display, click on the Service Tab under Anatomical Selector.

  4. On the Service Protocol Selection window, click on [MANUFACTURING].

  5. On the Service Category Protocol List, click on the [40.10 IMAGE SERIES 48CM] selection for 4/8 slice system; click on the [45.7 IMAGE SERIES 48CM] selection for 16 slice system.

  6. On the ExamRx protocol parameter display, select the 1st Series for 4/8 slice system (Series Description: QA1 MTF 2x10/120kV/260mA/1s);

    select the 3rd Series for 16 slice system(Series Description: 48 16x1.25/120kV/200mA/2s

  7. Set internal Landmark.
