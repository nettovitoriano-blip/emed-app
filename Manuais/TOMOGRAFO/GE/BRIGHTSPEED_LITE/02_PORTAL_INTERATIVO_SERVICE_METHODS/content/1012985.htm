2  Overview

Objective: The purpose of this test is to drive the rotation inverter in high speed mode on the tube in order to identify a defective rotation board or defective phase capacitors or a wrong connection among rotation board and tube. Summary: Run diagnostic. The test is executed without high voltage and filament heating, and needs no manual operation.

Last Revised:June 2003
