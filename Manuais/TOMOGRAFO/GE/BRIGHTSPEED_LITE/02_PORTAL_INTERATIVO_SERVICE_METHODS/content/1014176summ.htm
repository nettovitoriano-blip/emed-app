1  Procedure

1.1   Check Head Holder

  1. Check each head holder on site for the following:

    1. The latch hook stops at each of the lock and release positions.

    1. The hook axis has not become weak.

    1. No cracks exist on the acrylic resin parts; especially inspect well the rib root (where the patient’s neck is placed), and parts around the flat head screws. If any crack is found, replace the head holder.

    1. No flat head screws are loose. If loosened, tighten those. (Torque: 8 kg-cm; turn the grip of the screwdriver using thumb, index finger, and middle finger, which gives approximately this torque.) Tighten the loosened screws only, and DO NOT excessively tighten the screws, which will cause cracks.

    1. All velcro tape is securely attached to the head holders. If not the case, replace the velcro tape.

    1. Check that the lock hook is securely attached to each head holder. If not, tighten the loosened flat head screw. Do not over tighten screws.

    1. Repeat the same check for the cradle side lock hook.

1.2   Check Table moving parts Grounding Connections

  1. Tighten all electrical connections including grounding connections on the moving parts of the table.

1.3   Check Table Cover

  1. Check that the Table Cover is not deformed and not damaged. Also check that the Table Cover does not interfere with the cradle.

1.4   Check Gap Sponge

  1. Check that two pieces of sponge are securely attached to the Table front end. If they have come off, replace them.

  1. Check the gap between cradle and sponge is 7mm or less.

    (Step Details)

1.5   Gap between Table Cover and Cradle

  1. Check that the position of the table cover and the cradle are parallel, and the gap between them is 7mm or less.

1.6   Check Body Strap

  1. Move the Table to its lowest position.

  1. Install the phantom holder to the cradle, then set the 35cm phantom to the phantom holder.

  1. Using a packing tape or equivalent, hold the phantom to the phantom holder to prevent the phantom from dropping when lifting the cradle.

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach the left and right Body straps at 200 mm (7.9 in) position from the front end of the cradle.

    (Step Details)

  1. Lift up the cradle while pulling up the body strap.

  1. Verify that the body strap is NOT removed from the cradle.

  1. Check the other side of the body strap using steps 4 to 6.

    (Step Details)

1.9   Check Touch Sensor Operation

  1. Press the Touch Sensor without Table movement operations, and then verify that the Table will not move DOWN when Table DOWN SW is pressed.

  1. Repeat this for all touch sensors.

    (Step Details)

1.10   Clean Cradle tray / Table Cover

CAUTION
Use proper procedures to avoid blood-borne pathogens. The maintenance tasks which follow include procedures concerned with the cleaning of equipment (Table Tray, Gantry Cover, etc.). Refer to local / company guidelines for those procedures, especially where body fluids or other possible contaminates are present.

  1. Clean Table covers and cradle.

    (Step Details)

1.11   Check for Oil Leak

  1. Verify that no oil has leaked from the cylinder, pump, hoses, etc. Verify that the Table does not lower on its own when the Table is left unoperated for some time.

1.12   Grease-Up

  1. Remove the both sides Table covers and bottom Table covers.

  1. Apply grease (SHELL ALVANIA grease #2 or equivalent) to the following portion. (Pack grease between the inner and outer races of the ball bearing using your finger.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach the grease gun to the grease nipple of the linear guide and inject the grease.

  1. Wipe off any excess grease.
