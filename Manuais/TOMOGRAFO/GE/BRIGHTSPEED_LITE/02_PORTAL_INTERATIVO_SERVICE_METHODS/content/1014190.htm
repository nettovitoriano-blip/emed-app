Phase Shift Capacitors Set Replacement

(Click Here for Summary Version)

1  Personnel Requirements

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization
1Not Applicable30 minutes

2  Overview

Replace the phase shift capacitor set (Part no: 5175114). This capacitors set is attached on the auxiliary box

  • Remove straight cover of the auxiliary unit

  • Replace the Phase Shift Capacitors module

  • Replace cover

3  Preliminary Requirements

3.1  Safety

No general safety information.

3.2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Before beginning this procedure, please read the gantry safety information.

-

4  Procedure

4.1  Phase Shift Capacitors Set Removal

DANGER
BEFORE ANY MANUAL INTERVENTION, ENSURE THE MAIN POWER IS OFF. APPLY PROPER LOCK OUT - TAG OUT PROCEDURE FOR YOUR OWN SAFETY WHEN MANIPULATING INSIDE THE EQUIPMENT IS REQUIRED.

  1. Move table to its lowest elevation.

  1. Remove side, top and front gantry covers.

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove power at main disconnect panel. Use proper Lockout / Tagout procedures.

  1. Rotate the Gantry until the Auxiliary Assembly reaches the 3 o’clock position.

  1. Engage gantry rotational lock.

  1. Remove straight cover of the auxiliary box.

    1. Disconnect all the cables showed in below picture:

      Illustration 1:  Auxiliary Box Cable Connection

    2. Remove four screws and put the PI board assembly at proper position.

      Illustration 2:  Remove screws

    3. Remove ten (10) screws to remove the straight cover. Then the capacitors will appear.

      Illustration 3:  Remove the straight cover

      Illustration 4:  capacitors

  1. Disconnect the following cable from the Auxiliary Casting

    Illustration 5:  Disconnect cable from the Auxiliary Casting

  1. Unscrew the 8 screws around the plastic support, and remove the module from the Auxiliary Unit.

4.2  Phase Shift Capacitors Module Installation

  1. Ensure that Lockout / Tagout procedure has been applied, and that gantry power is removed.

  1. Ensure that gantry rotational lock is engaged and gantry does not rotate by attempting to rotate gantry by hand.

  1. Place the new phase shift capacitors module. Assemble the 8 Screws with respect of torques: 2Nm.

  1. Connect the cable to the Auxiliary Casting.

  1. Fasten the cables to the auxiliary box using ty-wraps.

  1. Install the straight cover of auxiliary box.

  1. Install the PI board assembly.

  1. Connect all the cables which are disconnect in previous step.

  1. Install all the gantry covers removed in previous steps.

5  Finalization

Run System Scanning Test verify that the system is operational.
