16 Slice DAS/Detector Troubleshooting with Crossover Tool

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

NOTE:The tool will not work on a DAS that uses elastomers. (4 and 8 slice scanners).

  1. Use DASTools to locate the bad/noisy channel. (See Illustration 3.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Physically remove the flex from the bad/noisy channel.

  1. Physically remove the flex from an adjacent good channel.

  1. Install one side of the Channel Crossover Tool to the Detector.

  1. Install the other side of the Channel Crossover Tool to the DAS. (SeeIllustration 4 and Illustration 5.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Use DASTools to see if bad channel moved, or stayed the same.

  1. If the bad channel indication moved 16 channels, the problem is with the Detector

  1. If the bad channel did not move, a DAS issue is indicated. Isolate the DAS issue. SeeIllustration 6 and Illustration 7.
