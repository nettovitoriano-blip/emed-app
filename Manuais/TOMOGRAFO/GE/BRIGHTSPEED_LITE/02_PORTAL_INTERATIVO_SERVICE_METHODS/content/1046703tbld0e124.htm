Workstation powered off automatically and the Power LED flashes red two times, once every second, followed by a two-second pause.Processor thermal protection activated

OR

Fan(s) blocked or not turning

OR

CPU heatsink fan assembly not properly attached to the processor
  1. Ensure that the Host PC air vents are not blocked.
  2. Verify that the cooling fan is running.
  3. Open the access panel, press the power button, and determine if the processor fan is spinning.
  4. If the processor fan is not spinning, ensure that the fan’s cable is plugged into the system board header.
  5. Ensure that the fan is properly seated/installed.
  6. Replace the Host PC if fans are not operational or if CPU heatsinks have been disturbed.
Power LED flashes red, once every two seconds.Power failure (power supply overloaded)
  1. Determine whether a device is causing the problem as follows:
    1. Remove all attached devices.
    2. Power on the system.
    3. If the system enters the POST, power off and replace one device at a time.
    4. Repeat this procedure until failure occurs.
    5. Replace the Host PC if any devices are found to be overloading the PSU.
  2. Verify power supply functionality as follows:
    1. Disconnect AC power.
    2. Remove all internal power supply cables from the system board.
    3. Plug in AC power and ensure that the power supply fan spins and the BIST LED lights.
    4. If the power supply fan does not spin or the BIST LED does not light, replace the Host PC.
