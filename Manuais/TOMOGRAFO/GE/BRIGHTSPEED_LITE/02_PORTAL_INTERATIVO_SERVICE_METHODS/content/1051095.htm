4  Procedure

  1. Shutdown system software in preparation to shutdown the system.

  1. Remove the right side cover and power down the gantry.

  1. Power down the entire system per LOTO procedures and wait 5 minutes to remove capacitive power.

  1. Remove the top and rear gantry covers.

  1. Remove the slipring safety cover that is over top of the HV connections for easier access to the HV terminals.

  1. Remove the damaged capacitor by removing the plastic shield, tywrap and screws as shown in Illustration 1 and Illustration 2.

    Illustration 1:  Capacitor removal part 1

    Illustration 2:  Capacitor removal part 2

  1. Install the new capacitor per the order below using Illustration 2 and Illustration 1 as a reference.

    • Install the capacitor per the above illustrations.

    • Install a ty-wrap around the two capacitors and HV cable ferite as shown in Illustration 1.

  1. Torque all screws to:

    Table 1:  Torque setting

    4 N-m35 lbs-in3 lbs-ft41 kg-cm

  1. Install the plastic shield as shown in Illustration 1. Torque the screws to the values shown in Table 1.

  1. Reinstall the slipring safety cover.

  1. Reinstall the gantry covers.

  1. Power on the gantry per standard procedures, wait for the detector to come up to operating temperature.

5  Finalization

  1. Perform any retests necessary as applicable to the failure mode originally seen.

  1. Perform a generic service scan with the QA phantom to look for any artifacts.
