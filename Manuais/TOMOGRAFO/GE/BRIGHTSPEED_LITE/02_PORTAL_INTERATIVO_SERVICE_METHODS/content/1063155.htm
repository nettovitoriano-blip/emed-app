4  Procedure

4.1  Phantom Setup

  1. Record phantom serial number in the appropriate GE e4879 Installation form, GE e4879 Certified Component Verification form, or GE Data Report Planned Maintenance Schedule form.

  1. Perform Center Phantom

  1. Set Internal Landmark.

4.2  Standard Test

4.2.1  Acquire Images

  1. Complete Phantom Setup (See Section 4.1).

  1. Select [NEW PATIENT].

  1. Type the following entries in the listed Patient Information fields:

    1. Patient ID: Service

    1. Patient Name: Quality Assurance Test

  1. Select Service from Protocol Selection, Anatomical Selector.

  1. Click [CONFIRM] to scan Series 1, Series Description: QA1 4x5/120kV/575mA/1s prescription.

  1. Press [START] on SCIM/GSCB when it flashes.

  1. Click [NEXT SERIES] when Series 1 scans are completed.

  1. Click [CONFIRM] to scan Series 2, Series Description: QA3 Heli/0.516/120kV/135mA/1s prescription.

  1. Press [MOVE TO SCAN] on SCIM/GSCB when it flashes.

  1. Press [START] on SCIM/GSCB when it flashes.

  1. Click [END EXAM].

  1. If PPS Response window is displayed, click [COMPLETE].

4.2.2  Analysis

  1. Select [SERVICE] desktop and move Service desktop to left monitor.

  1. Select [IMAGE QUALITY] from Service desktop.

  1. Select [IMAGE ANALYSIS] and move Image Analysis window to left monitor.

  1. Select scanned Examination Number, Series Number 1 and Image Number 1 from right monitor List/Select browser.

  1. Click [AUTO 1X] radio button on Image Analysis window.

  1. Click [AUTO 1X] selection button, and select [IMGSER 20QA], [QA1 4x5/120kv/57mA/1sec] from pull down menus.

  1. Click [ACCEPT].

    NOTE:At test completion, a Report window is displayed, which includes specification limits and Pass/Fail results.

    NOTE:Test Pass/Fail summary is posted at top of Report window.

    NOTE:If test fails, repeat test. If test fails persistently, perform appropriate corrective action.

  1. Click [DISMISS] in Report window.

  1. Select scanned Examination Number, Series Number 3 and Image Number 1 from right monitor List/Select browser.

  1. Click Image Analysis [AUTO 1X] selection button, and select [IMGSER 20QA], [QA3 Heli/0.516/120kv/135mA/1sec], [AUTO] from pull down menus.

  1. Click [ACCEPT].

    NOTE:At test completion, a Report window is displayed, which includes specification limits and Pass/Fail results.

    NOTE:Test Pass/Fail summary is posted at top of Report window.

    NOTE:If test fails, repeat test. If test fails persistently, perform appropriate corrective action.

  1. Click [DISMISS] in Report window.

  1. Click [QUIT] in Image Analysis window.

5  Finalization

  1. Record Standard Test Pass/Fail summary in the appropriate GE e4879 Installation form, GE e4879 Certified Component Verification form, or GE Data Report Planned Maintenance Schedule form.

  1. Store the appropriate GE e4879 Installation form, GE e4879 Certified Component Verification form, or GE Data Report Planned Maintenance Schedule form with system history records.

  1. Store the Standard Test Quality Assurance results with system history records. The results are stored in a text file on the scanner and can be retrieved at /usr/g/service/state/image_analysis.log.
