HP Host Computer Hard Drive Exchange

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

NOTICE
Potential for Equipment Damage.

This optional procedure can be used if the failure that required replacement of the host computer, is not hard drive related. All drives must be in working condition and exchanged at once.

 

NOTICE
To prevent damage to components within the HP xw8x00 chassis, observe the following ESD precautions:

  • Work on a static-free mat.

  • Wear a static strap to ensure that any accumulated electrostatic charge is discharged from your body to ground.

  • Create a common ground for the equipment you are working on by connecting the static-free mat, static strap and peripheral units to that piece of equipment.

 

1  Procedure

1.1   Remove the Side Cover

(For GOC Console: All cables removed from the Computer) (For  TIO/NIO Console: Not need to remove all cables from the Computer) perform the following steps:

  1. Unlock the cover on the side of the old Host Computer.

    (Step Details)

  1. Pull out on the side cover latch to release the side cover.

  1. Tilt the cover open, then lift it off. Refer to Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Repeat the above procedure, to open the new Host Computer.

1.2   Remove the Hard Drives

  1. Place the Host Computer on its side with the system board facing up.

  1. Label each SCSI connection as follows:

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the power and SAS data cables from each drive.

  1. Place your fingers on the colored release clips on the sides of the drive, and squeeze inward. Pull gently just enough to release the drive rail latches. Refer to Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
The drive rails are not screwed on to the drive. Do not hold the drive by the rails when it is not installed in a drive bay, because you could drop and damage the drive.
 

  1. Grasp the hard drive with your hand and pull out.

  1. Place the hard drive (with rails attached) on a static mat.

NOTICE
Mark or label each drive (i.e. Slot 1-4) so they are re-installed in the correct slot in the new Host Computer.
 

  1. Repeat the above procedures to extract all hard drives from the old (failed) Host Computer.

  1. Repeat the above procedures on the new Host Computer to extract all hard drives.

1.3   Install the Hard Drives

NOTICE
Hold onto the hard drive - NOT the rails - when handling the drive.
 

  1. Insert the site's original drives into the exact same slots of the new Host Computer.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the drive cables:

    1. Connect the proper power cable to each hard drive.

    1. Connect the proper SAS Data cable to all hard disk drives. Refer to below description of SAS cables:

      (Step Details)

  1. Repeat the above procedures on the old (failed) Host Computer to install all hard drives removed from the new Host Computer.

    (Step Details)

1.4   Replace the Side Cover

  1. Ensure that all internal cables are properly connected and safely routed in the new Host Computer.

  1. Place the side cover onto the Host Computer chassis (aligning the guide rail on the bottom inside edge of the cover with the bottom edge of the Host Computer chassis). Refer to Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Verify no cables are pinched, and gently close the cover.

  1. Lock the cover using the key provided.

  1. Repeat the above procedure, to close and lock the original Host Computer, with the new drives installed.

2  Finalization

  1. Continue with HP Host Computer Replacement (if applicable).
