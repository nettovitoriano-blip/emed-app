HP Host Computer Hard Drive Replacement (xw8400/xw8600)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

NOTICE
To prevent damage to components within the HP xw8x00 chassis, observe the following ESD precautions:

  • Work on a static-free mat.

  • Wear a static strap to ensure that any accumulated electrostatic charge is discharged from your body to ground.

  • Create a common ground for the equipment you are working on by connecting the static-free mat, static strap and peripheral units to that piece of equipment.

 

1  Procedure

1.1   Remove the Side Cover

(For  HP xw8400: All cables removed from the Computer) (For  TIO Console: Not need to remove all cables from the Computer) perform the following steps:

  1. Unlock the cover on the side of the Host Computer.

    (Step Details)

  1. Pull out on the side cover latch to release the side cover.

  1. Tilt the cover open, then lift it off. Refer to Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

1.2   Remove the Hard Drives

  1. The Hard disk Drives are defined as below. Identify the Hard Disk Drive which you want to replace:

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the power and SAS data cable from the hard disk drive which will be replaced.

  1. Place your fingers on the colored release clips on the sides of the drive, and squeeze inward. Pull gently just enough to release the drive rail latches. Refer to Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
The drive rails are not screwed on to the drive. Do not hold the drive by the rails when it is not installed in a drive bay, because you could drop and damage the drive.
 

  1. Grasp the hard drive with your hand and pull out.

  1. Place the hard drive (with rails attached) on a static mat.

  1. Remove the rail from the hard disk drive, and put it aside. Refer to Illustration 3

    (Step Details)

1.3   Install the Hard Drives

NOTICE
Hold onto the hard drive - NOT the rails - when handling the drive.
 

  1. Install the rail to new hard disk drive.

  1. Insert the new hard disk drive in the same slot of Host Computer.

  1. Connect the SAS cable and power cable to hard disk drive.

    1. Connect the power cable to hard disk drive.

    1. Connect the proper SAS Data cable to hard disk drive. Refer to below description of SAS cable:

      (Step Details)

  1. Repeat the above procedures to install all hard drives in case multiple hard disk drives are replaced.

    (Step Details)

1.4   Replace the Side Cover

  1. Ensure that all internal cables are properly connected and safely routed in the Host Computer.

  1. Place the side cover onto the Host Computer chassis (aligning the guide rail on the bottom inside edge of the cover with the bottom edge of the Host Computer chassis). Refer to Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Verify no cables are pinched, and gently close the cover.

  1. Lock the cover using the key provided.

  1. Repeat the above procedure, to close and lock the Host Computer, with the new drives installed.

1.5   Perform LFC to load software

Please refer to LFC procedure to load all the software and options.

1.6   Retro Recon Test

The Reconstruction (not Scan) portion of the system can be tested at this point.

  1. Click on the <RETRO RECON>selection on the left Monitor.

  1. Click the <RAT GOLD> series.

  1. Click <SELECT SERIES>.

  1. Click on <CONFIRM>.

  1. Verify the image(s) reconstruct and are displayed.

  1. Click on <QUIT>.

2  Finalization

  1. Take some scans, verify the image(s) reconstruct and are displayed.

  1. Check that all customer functions are operational. (Auto voice, filming, network, archive.)

  1. Install the side Operator Console covers.
