HP Host Computer CDIP/CDIP2 Board Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

CAUTION
Potential for injury.
The HP Computer module weighs approximately 20.5kg (45 lbs).
Use care, when removing the HP module.

NOTICE
To prevent damage to components within the HP xw8x00 chassis, observe the following ESD precautions:

  • Work on a static-free mat.

  • Wear a static strap to ensure that any accumulated electrostatic charge is discharged from your body to ground.

  • Create a common ground for the equipment you are working on by connecting the static-free mat, static strap and peripheral units to that piece of equipment.

 

1  Procedure

1.1   Power OFF (Shut Down) the Console

  1. If Applications are up, click on the [Shut Down] button.

  1. Power OFF the Operator Console front switch, when instructed to “Power Down”.

  1. Remove the rear Operator Console cover.

  1. Visually verify proper labeling of each cable and disconnect all HP Host Computer rear cables.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the front Operator Console cover.

  1. Remove and retain the four (4) front mounting screws.

CAUTION
Potential for injury.
The HP Computer module weighs approximately 20.5kg (45 lbs).
Use care, when removing the HP module.

  1. Slide out the HP Host Computer module.

1.2   Remove the Side Cover

In All-in-One console, CDIP/CDIP2 board is installed in HP host computer. Before CDIP/CDIP2 board replacement, need to open side cover of HP host computer at first. With ALL cables removed from the old and new Host Computers, perform the following steps:

  1. Unlock the cover on the side of the old Host Computer.

    (Step Details)

  1. Pull out on the side cover latch to release the side cover.

  1. Tilt the cover open, then lift it off. Refer to Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

1.3   CDIP/CDIP2 board replacement

  1. Locate the CDIP/CDIP2 board in Host Computer. CDIP/CDIP2 board is installed in slot 5.

  1. Remove the old CDIP/CDIP2 board from Host Computer and install new CDIP/CDIP2 board in the same slot position (slot 5) in Host Computer.

1.4   Replace the Side Cover

  1. Ensure that all internal cables are properly connected and safely routed in the new Host Computer.

  1. Place the side cover onto the Host Computer chassis (aligning the guide rail on the bottom inside edge of the cover with the bottom edge of the Host Computer chassis). Refer to Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Verify no cables are pinched, and gently close the cover.

  1. Lock the cover using the key provided.

1.5   Install the New HP Host Computer

  1. Slide in the HP Host Computer module.

  1. Replace the four (4) front mounting screws.

  1. Reconnect all HP Host Computer rear cables.

NOTE:In this case, if CDIP2 replace old CDIP, need replace the fiber cable which from bulkhead to host computer rear panel.

  1. At the rear of the HP Host Computer, verify the power switch is in the ON position.

  1. Apply power to the Operator Console by turning ON the front switch.

2  Finalization

  1. Take some scans, verify the image(s) reconstruct and are displayed.

  1. Install the front and rear Operator Console covers.
