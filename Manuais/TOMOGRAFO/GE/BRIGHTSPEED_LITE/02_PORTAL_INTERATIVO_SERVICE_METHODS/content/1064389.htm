4  Procedure

4.1  24 VDC Supply Replacement

  1. (For Removal)

    1. Make sure to follow all Lockout/Tagout requirements while performing this procedure. Refer to “Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE” procedure in Safety folder of publication.

    1. Remove the Top Cover and Front Cover of the PDU.

    1. Remove the front plastic safety shield.

    1. Remove the connectors from the 24 VDC Power Supply (located in the lower right corner of the PDU).

    1. Remove the two screws holding the 24 VDC Power Supply in place.

    1. Remove the 24 VDC Power Supply from the PDU.

  1. (For Re-Install)

    1. Install the new 24 VDC Power Supply in the PDU and secure it with the screws removed earlier.

    1. Attach the connectors to the replacement 24 VDC Power Supply.

    1. Install the front plastic safety shield.

    1. Install the PDU Front Cover and Top Cover.

    1. Remove all Lockout/tagout devices installed.

    1. Restore power to the system. Refer to “Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE” procedure in Safety folder of publication.

4.2  Capacitor Replacement

  1. (For Removal)

    1. Make sure to follow all Lockout/Tagout requirements while performing this procedure. Refer to “Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE” procedure in Safety folder of publication.

    1. Remove the Top Cover and Front Cover of the PDU.

    1. Remove the front plastic safety shield.

    1. Remove the plate on top of the capacitors by removing the eight screws and connectors.

      NOTE:Observe capacitor polarity and connector alignment.

    1. Loosen the clamping screw holding the capacitor in place.

    1. Slide the capacitor out towards the front of the PDU.

  1. (For Re-Install)

    1. Slide in the new capacitor.

    1. Tighten the clamping screw around the new capacitor to hold it in place.

    1. Install the plate over the capacitors using the 8 screws previously removed, attaching all connectors to their original connections.

    1. Install the front plastic safety shield.

    1. Install the PDU Front Cover and Top Cover.

    1. Remove all Lockout/tagout devices installed.

    1. Restore power to the system. Refer to “Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE” procedure in Safety folder of publication.

4.6  E-Stop Switch Replacement

    1. Remove the two screws holding the E-Stop Switch mount to the chassis.

  1. (For Re-Install)

    1. Install the new E-Stop Switch.

    1. Tighten the housing to hold the E-Stop Switch in place.

    1. Connect the wires to the E-Stop Switch.

    1. Install the switch mount using the two screws removed earlier.

    1. Install the front plastic safety shield. (Also install top plastic cover if it was removed.)

    1. Install the PDU Front Cover and Top Cover.

    1. Remove all Lockout/tagout devices installed.

    1. Restore power to the system. Refer to “Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE” procedure in Safety folder of publication.

4.9  PDU Control Board Replacement

  1. (For Removal)

    1. Make sure to follow all Lockout/Tagout requirements while performing this procedure. Refer to “Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE” procedure in Safety folder of publication.

    1. Remove the Top Cover and Front Cover of the PDU.

    1. Remove the front plastic safety shield.

    1. Remove the plug connectors from the PDU Control Board (located in the upper right corner of the compartment).

    1. Remove the cable connector at the bottom of the PDU Control Board.

    1. Remove the seven screws holding the PDU Control Board in place.

    1. Remove the PDU Control Board from the PDU.

  1. (For Re-Install)

    1. Install the PDU Control Board in the PDU and secure it with the screws removed earlier.

    1. Attach the cable connector to the replacement PDU Control Board.

    1. Attach the plugs into the connectors on the replacement PDU Control Board.

    1. Install the front plastic safety shield.

    1. Install the PDU Front Cover and Top Cover.

    1. Remove all Lockout/tagout devices installed.

    1. Restore power to the system. Refer to “Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE” procedure in Safety folder of publication.
