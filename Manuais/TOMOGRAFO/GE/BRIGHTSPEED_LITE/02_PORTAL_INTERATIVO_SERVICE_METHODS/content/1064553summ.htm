Securing HV Cable

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Power to the system should be turned off.

-

-

Gantry side covers, gantry top covers, and gantry front cover should be removed.

-

-

Recommend procedure completion by GE Service trained personnel.

-

-

2  Procedure

  1. With Dry Wipes, soak up the oil in the High Voltage Tank receptacle.

    (Step Details)

  1. Open syringe package from piston side (avoid any contact with other tip).

    (Step Details)

  1. Fill up syringe with 18 ml oil from oil can.

  1. Hold the syringe at an angle to the receptacle as shown in Illustration 3 and pump 18 ml oil on to the receptacle wall.

    (Step Details)

  1. Discard the syringe properly.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install O-Ring

    (Step Details)

  1. With Tube High Voltage cable connector key facing front of gantry, slowly insert Tube High Voltage cable, engaging connector pins, and seat cable in High Voltage Tank receptacle (Illustration 5).

    (Step Details)

  1. Hand tighten cable-locking ring.

  1. Rotate cable strain relief for a clean cable dress.

  1. Use spanner wrench to tighten cable-locking ring.

CAUTION
Incorrectly sealed and secured High Voltage cable can result in damage to High Voltage Tank, Tube, or other parts of gantry. Do not over-tighten cable-locking ring. Over-tightening can deform cable plug sealing surfaces, break oil seal between High Voltage Tank receptacle and housing, twisted receptacle, and disrupt internal wiring. Wash hands to clean off oil.

  1. Carefully wipe up all excess oil.

  1. Position High Voltage cable into each High Voltage Inverter and Auxiliary Box clamp.

  1. Secure High Voltage Cable with Ty-Wraps

    Illustration 6:  Correctly Secured HV Cable

  1. Add paper napkin to candle stick entrance and secure with Ty-wraps, as shown in Illustration 7.

    (Step Details)

3  Finalization

  1. Disengage rotational lock if engaged.

  1. Rotate Tube to 6 o'clock position. Check for oil leaks and carefully wipe up any excess oil.

  1. Rotate Tube to 3 o'clock position. Check for oil leaks and carefully wipe up any excess oil.
