2  Overview

The System Scanning Test provides the necessary steps to exercise the system and to ensure system integrity before releasing.

4  Procedure

4.1  Phantom Setup

  1. Record phantom serial number on System Scanning Test Report.

  1. Perform Center Phantom.

  1. Set Internal Landmark.

4.2  Acquire Scans

  1. Select [NEW PATIENT].

  1. Type following entries in listed Patient Information fields:

    1. Patient ID: Service

    1. Patient Name: System Scanning Test

  1. Select Service from Protocol Selection, Anatomical Selector.

  1. Select [MANUFACTURING].

    1. If [SERVICE SYSTEM SCANNING TEST] exists, perform Using Service System Scanning Test Protocol.

    1. If [SERVICE SYSTEM SCANNING TEST] does not exist, perform Using System Scan/Cust Qual Reli Protocol.

    NOTE:Not all software releases have the protocol as of the release of this procedure.

4.2.1  Using Service System Scanning Test Protocol

  1. Select [SERVICE SYSTEM SCANNING TEST].

  1. Click [CONFIRM].

  1. Press [MOVE TO SCAN] on SCIM when it flashes.

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes. First Scout scans are performed.

  1. Press [MOVE TO SCAN] on SCIM when it flashes.

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes. Second Scout scans are performed.

  1. Click [NEXT SERIES]

  1. Click [CONFIRM].

  1. Press [MOVE TO SCAN] and [PRESCRIBED TILT] on SCIM when they flash.

    NOTE:Hold [PRESCRIBED TILT] until tilt position is reached.

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes. Axial scans are performed.

  1. Click [NEXT SERIES]

  1. Click [CONFIRM].

  1. Press [MOVE TO SCAN] and [PRESCRIBED TILT] on SCIM when they flash.

    NOTE:Hold [PRESCRIBED TILT] until tilt position is reached.

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes. Helical scans are performed.

  1. Click [END EXAM].

  1. If PPS Response window is displayed, click [COMPLETE].

  1. Skip to Section 4.3.

4.2.2  Using System Scan/Cust Qual Reli Protocol

  1. Select [SYSTEM SCAN/CUST QUAL RELI].

  1. Modify Series 1 - Scout as follows:

    NOTE:All Scout protocols are modified.

    1. Click [START LOC].

    1. Enter S80 and press [ENTER].

    1. Click [END LOC].

    1. Enter I80 and press [ENTER].

  1. Click [CONFIRM].

  1. Press [MOVE TO SCAN] on SCIM when it flashes.

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes. First Scout scans are performed.

  1. Press [MOVE TO SCAN] on SCIM when it flashes.

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes. Second Scout scans are performed.

  1. Click [NEXT SERIES]

  1. Delete Groups 3 (Images 65-96) and 4 (Images 97-128) of Series 2.

    1. Select Images 97 - 128 and click [DELETE SELECTED GROUP].

    1. Select Images 65 - 96, if not already selected, and click [DELETE SELECTED GROUP].

  1. Modify Series 2 - Axial as follows:

    NOTE:All Axial protocols are modified.

    1. Click [GANTRY TILT].

    1. Enter I15 and press [ENTER].

    1. Click [START LOC].

    1. Enter S65 and press [ENTER].

    1. Click [END LOC].

    1. Enter I15.235 and press [ENTER].

  1. Click [CONFIRM].

  1. Press [MOVE TO SCAN] and [PRESCRIBED TILT] on SCIM when they flash.

    NOTE:Hold [PRESCRIBED TILT] until tilt position is reached.

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes. Axial scans are performed.

  1. Click [NEXT SERIES]

  1. Modify Series 3 - Helical as follows:

    NOTE:All Helical protocols are modified.

    1. Click [START LOC].

    1. Enter S90 and press [ENTER].

    1. Click [END LOC].

    1. Enter I40 and press [ENTER].

    1. Click [NO. OF IMAGES].

    1. Enter 27 and press [ENTER].

  1. Click [CONFIRM].

  1. Press [MOVE TO SCAN] and [PRESCRIBED TILT] on SCIM when they flash.

    NOTE:Hold [PRESCRIBED TILT] until tilt position is reached.

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes. Helical scans are performed.

  1. Click [END EXAM].

  1. If PPS Response window is displayed, click [COMPLETE].

4.3  Image Review

  1. Select ImageWorks desktop.

  1. Select the scanned examination from List/Select browser.

  1. Click [VIEWER] and visually review all images from for unusual artifacts (rings, streaks, shading, cupping, noise, or center artifacts).

    NOTE:If any artifacts are identified, perform appropriate corrective action to resolve problem and retest.

  1. Record results on System Scanning Test Report and store with system history records.
