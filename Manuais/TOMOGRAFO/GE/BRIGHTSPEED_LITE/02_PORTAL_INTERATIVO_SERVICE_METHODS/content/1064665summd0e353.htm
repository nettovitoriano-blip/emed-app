NOTE:To eject the MOD disk from the MOD drive, it must first be "detached" via [Detach] in the Archive menu on the browser. If the MOD fails to detach and cannot be ejected, click [Shutdown] in the upper right corner of the right monitor. When you see the "OK to power off the system" message, press [Eject] on the MOD drive. The MOD will now eject.
