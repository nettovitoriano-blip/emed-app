PDU Covers Remove/Install

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
ELECTROCUTION HAZARD.
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
USE PROPER LOCKOUT / TAGOUT PROCEDURES BEFORE REPLACING THE COMPONENTS.

NOTICE
Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.
 

1  Procedure

1.1   PDU Top Cover Removal / Installation

1.1.1  PDU Top Cover Removal

  1. Make sure to follow all Lockout/Tagout requirements while performing this procedure.

  1. Using a screwdriver loosen the screw holding the PDU Top Cover in place.

  1. Remove the PDU Top Cover.

1.1.2  PDU Top Cover Installation

  1. Make sure to follow all Lockout/Tagout requirements while performing this procedure.

  1. Install the PDU Top Cover.

  1. Tighten the screw holding the PDU Top Cover in place.

1.2   PDU Front Cover Removal / Installation

1.2.1  PDU Front Cover Removal

  1. Make sure to follow all Lockout/Tagout requirements while performing this procedure.

  1. Using a screwdriver loosen the screw holding the PDU Top Cover in place.

  1. Remove the PDU Top Cover.

  1. Remove the two screws holding the PDU Front Cover in place.

  1. Lift the PDU Front Cover up slightly until it clears the latches and set it aside.

1.2.2  PDU Front Cover Installation

  1. Make sure to follow all Lockout/Tagout requirements while performing this procedure.

  1. Place the PDU Front Cover in its latches and slide the cover down.

  1. Tighten the two screws holding the PDU Front Cover in place.

  1. Install the PDU Top Cover and tighten the screw holding it in place.

1.3   PDU Back Cover Removal / Installation

1.3.1  PDU Back Cover Removal

  1. Make sure to follow all Lockout/Tagout requirements while performing this procedure.

  1. Using a screwdriver loosen the screw holding the PDU Top Cover in place.

  1. Remove the PDU Top Cover.

  1. Remove the two screws holding the PDU Back Cover in place.

  1. Lift the PDU Back Cover up slightly until it clears the latches and set it aside.

1.3.2  PDU Back Cover Installation

  1. Make sure to follow all Lockout/Tagout requirements while performing this procedure.

  1. Place the PDU Back Cover in its latches and slide the cover down.

  1. Tighten the two screws holding the PDU Back Cover in place.

  1. Install the PDU Top Cover and tighten the screw holding it in place.

1.4   PDU Side Cover Removal/Installation

1.4.1  PDU Side Cover Removal

  1. Make sure to follow all Lockout/Tagout requirements while performing this procedure.

  1. Remove the PDU top, front, and back covers.

  1. Remove the two screws from the bottom front and bottom back of the PDU.

  1. Lift the side cover up and remove from the PDU.

1.4.2  PDU Side Cover Installation

  1. Make sure to follow all Lockout/Tagout requirements while performing this procedure.

  1. Place PDU side cover in its latches and slide cover down.

  1. Tighten the two screws on the bottom front and bottom back of the PDU.

  1. Install the PDU top, front, and back covers.
