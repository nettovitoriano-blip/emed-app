2  Overview

This procedure defines the replacement process for the Integrated Peripheral Controller (IPC) Board.

3.3  Safety

You must wear a grounded ESD wrist strap. Place removed electronic parts on or in an antistatic surface.
 

NOTICE
Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.
 

4  Procedure

  1. Make sure to follow all Lockout/Tagout requirements while performing this procedure. Refer to Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE procedure.

  1. Remove Gantry Base Covers as needed to access the IPC board.

  1. Disconnect all connections to the IPC Board.

  1. Remove the six bolts attaching the IPC Board to the Gantry.

  1. Set the IPC Board aside.

  1. Use the six bolts previously removed to attach the replacement IPC Board onto the Gantry.

  1. Reconnect all connections to the IPC Board.

  1. Remove all installed Lockout/tagout devices.

  1. Restore power to the system. Refer to Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE procedure.

5  Finalization

  1. To make sure the IPC board is functioning, an LED inspection should be made on the board.
