Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization when working on this product.

3  Procedure

NOTICE
Potential For Equipment Damage. DO NOT lift the Collimator by the Motors.
 

NOTE:Please check the component P/N when replace the collimator related parts, should be ordered the same P/N according to the label on each component.

  1. Move table to its lowest elevation.

  1. Remove gantry covers as required.

    (Step Details)

  1. Position the tube at 3 o’clock.

DANGER
Electrocution hazard
High Voltages present.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

  1. Turn OFF all three (3) gantry service switches (Axial Drive Enable, HVDC Enable, 120 VAC). Perform Lockout/Tagout procedures to remove all power from gantry.

DANGER
CRUSH HAZARD.
UNEXPECTED GANTRY ROTATION CAN CAUSE SERIOUS INJURY.
Never service the gantry with the axial drive enabled. Use the gantry rotation lock to lock out gantry rotation. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

  1. Engage gantry rotational lock.

WARNING
severe injury possible.
if the hoist hook's lock nut is loose, then the collimator might drop from the hoist, with the resulting in serious injury to service personnel.
before using the hoist, check to make certain that the hook's lock nut is not loose. If it is loose, do not use the hoist. instead, order a new hoist.

  1. Connect and secure hoist and tube change bracket to the tube.

  1. Remove high voltage cables from cathode and anode using spanner wrench.

  1. Remove the tube by following the procedure in Tube Replacement.

NOTICE
Be careful to keep tube and oil droplets away from contaminating the slipring.
 

  1. Disconnect connectors J2, J3 and J11 from collimator.

  1. Remove collimator by removing the two (2) mounting bolts from the top, two (2) bolts from the bottom, and 2 bolts from the rear of the collimator.

  1. Install new collimator by replacing the six (6) bolts removed in above step.

      (Step Details)

    1. Apply Final torque to M14 Mounting bolts:

      (Step Details)

  1. Secure the tube back in place.

  1. Replace high voltage cables back using spanner wrench.

    (Step Details)

  1. Reassemble gantry.

  1. Perform ISO Alignment Procedure.

  1. Perform Hot ISO Alignment.

  1. Perform Collimator Calibration.

  1. Perform Detailed Cals.

  1. Perform N Number Check.

  1. Perform System Scanning Test.

  1. Perform Quality Assurance Test.

  1. Perform Save System State.
