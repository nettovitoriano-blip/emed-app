System Scanning Test (BrightSpeed)

1  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Recommend procedure completion by trained personnel.

-

-

2  Procedure

2.1   Phantom Setup

  1. Record phantom serial number on System Scanning Test Report.

  1. Perform Center Phantom.

  1. Set Internal Landmark.

2.2   Acquire Scans

  1. Select [NEW PATIENT] from bottom of Scan Monitor.

  1. Type following entries in listed Patient Information fields:

    1. Patient ID: Service

    1. Patient Name: System Scanning Test

  1. Select Service tab from Protocol Selection, Anatomical Selector.

  1. Select [MANUFACTURING] from Service tab.

    1. If [SERVICE SYSTEM SCANNING TEST] exists, perform Using Service System Scanning Test Protocol.

    1. If [SERVICE SYSTEM SCANNING TEST] does not exist, perform Using System Scan/Cust Qual Reli Protocol.

    (Step Details)

2.2.1  Using Service System Scanning Test Protocol

  1. Select [SERVICE SYSTEM SCANNING TEST] from [MANUFACTURING].

  1. Press [CONFIRM].

  1. Press [MOVE TO SCAN] on SCIM when it flashes.

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes. First Scout scans are performed.

  1. Press [MOVE TO SCAN] on SCIM when it flashes.

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes. Second Scout scans are performed.

  1. Press [NEXT SERIES]

  1. Press [CONFIRM].

  1. Press [MOVE TO SCAN] and [PRESCRIBED TILT] on SCIM when they flash.

    (Step Details)

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes. Axial scans are performed.

  1. Press [NEXT SERIES]

  1. Press [CONFIRM].

  1. Press [MOVE TO SCAN] and [PRESCRIBED TILT] buttons on SCIM when they flash.

    (Step Details)

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes. Helical scans are performed.

  1. Select [END EXAM].

  1. If a PPS Response window is displayed, select [COMPLETE].

2.2.2  Using System Scan/Cust Qual Reli Protocol

  1. Select [SYSTEM SCAN/CUST QUAL RELI] from [MANUFACTURING].

  1. Modify Series 1 - Scout as follows:

    NOTE:All Scout protocols are modified.

    1. Click [START LOC].

    1. Enter S80 and press [ENTER].

    1. Click [END LOC].

    1. Enter I80 and press [ENTER].

  1. Press [CONFIRM].

  1. Press [MOVE TO SCAN] on SCIM when it flashes.

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes. First Scout scans are performed.

  1. Press [MOVE TO SCAN] on SCIM when it flashes.

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes. Second Scout scans are performed.

  1. Press [NEXT SERIES]

  1. Delete Groups 3 and 4 of Series 2.

    1. Select Groups 4 and click [DELETE SELECTED GROUP].

    1. Select Groups 3, if not already selected, and click [DELETE SELECTED GROUP].

  1. Modify Series 2 - Axial as follows:

    NOTE:All Axial protocols are modified.

    1. Click [GANTRY TILT].

    1. Enter I15 and press [ENTER].

    1. Click [END LOC].

    1. Enter I12.130 in Group 1.

    1. Enter I17.308 in Group 2.

  1. Press [CONFIRM].

  1. Press [MOVE TO SCAN] and [PRESCRIBED TILT] on SCIM when they flash.

    (Step Details)

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes. Axial scans are performed.

  1. Press [NEXT SERIES]

  1. Modify Series 3 - Helical as follows:

    NOTE:All Helical protocols are modified.

    1. Click [START LOC].

    1. Enter S70 and press [ENTER].

  1. Press [CONFIRM].

  1. Press [MOVE TO SCAN] and [PRESCRIBED TILT] on SCIM when they flash.

    (Step Details)

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes. Helical scans are performed.

  1. Select [END EXAM].

  1. If a PPS Response window is displayed, select [COMPLETE].

2.3   Image Review

  1. From ImageWorks desktop, review all images from exam for unusual artifacts (rings, streaks, shading, cupping, noise, or center artifacts).

    (Step Details)

  1. Record results on System Scanning Test Report and store with system history records.
