2  Procedure

2.1   Phantom Setup

  1. Install all gantry covers and scan window.

  1. Move table to home longitudinal position.

  1. Attach phantom holder to gantry end of cradle.

  1. Mount and center phantom to phantom holder.

  1. Place bubble level on top of phantom, positioned along the Z-axis. Adjust phantom holder back knob until phantom is level front to back.

  1. Turn on laser alignment lights.

  1. Move table into gantry until axial internal alignment lights are lined up with phantom's axial reference mark.

  1. On the console, select [SCANNER UTILITIES] from the bottom of the Scan Monitor.

  1. Select [CENTER PHANTOM] from the Scanner Utilities window.

  1. Select [CONFIRM] in the Phantom Centering window.

  1. Press [START]] on SCIM when it flashes. A Phantom Centering scan is performed.

  1. Adjust phantom position if results are out of specification range.

    (Step Details)

  1. Repeat steps [10] - [12] until phantom is centered.

  1. Once phantom is centered, press [DONE] in the Phantom Centering window.

  1. Press [QUIT] in the Scanner Utilities window.

  1. Set Internal Landmark.

2.2   Acquire Scans

  1. Click [NEW PATIENT] from the bottom of the Scan Monitor.

  1. Type the following entries in the listed Patient Information fields:

    1. Patient ID: Service

    1. Patient Name: Streak Test

  1. From the Protocol Selection display, click on the Anatomical Selector Service tab.

  1. On the Service Protocol Selection window, select [IMAGE QUALITY], [44.1 STREAK TEST].

    (Step Details)

  1. Select [CONFIRM]. An Attention message box is displayed.

  1. Click [CONTINUE] in the Attention message box.

  1. Press [START] on the SCIM when it flashes. Scans are performed.

  1. When all the scans are completed, click [END EXAM].

  1. If a PPS Response window is displayed, click [COMPLETE].

2.3   Image Review

  1. From the [IMAGEWORKS] desktop List/Select browser, select the scanned examination.

  1. Select [VIEWER] and review all images from the scanned examination for streak artifacts.

  1. If any images depict streaks, perform the steps in Section 4.4 on those images.

2.4   Streak Analysis

  1. Select [SERVICE] desktop and move the Service Desktop window to the left monitor.

  1. Select [IMAGE QUALITY] from the Service Desktop.

  1. Select [IMAGE ANALYSIS] and move [IMAGE ANALYSIS] window to the left monitor.

  1. From the List/Select browser on the right monitor, select the image to be analyzed.

  1. Click the[MANUAL] radio button on the Image Analysis window.

  1. Click the [MANUAL] selection button, and select [STREAK].

  1. Click [ACCEPT]. The image is displayed in the Image Analysis window with a Streak ROI.

  1. Follow instructions, in Image Analysis window lower right pane, to size Streak ROI and analyze.

  1. From the results, if the Measured Streak Factor is out of the specification range of +/-4.0, perform appropriate corrective action.

  1. When all tests are completed, click [QUIT].
