1. Turn on the gantry 120VAC service switch and press the table drives enable at the lower right corner of the service switch panel.

  2. Turn on service mode switch S4 (up position)

  3. Turn on the tilt enable switch S9 (on position)

  4. Use the tilt switch S10 to tilt the gantry forward. Look to see that you have access to the sensor board.

  5. Turn off the gantry 120VAC service switch.
