True-In-One Console Cover Removal and Installation

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Side Covers

1.1.1  Removal Procedure

  1. Remove the four screws at the side cover. See Illustration 1

    Illustration 1:  Side Cover Removal

  1. Lift cover away, so that tabs disengage from bottom lip of opening

1.1.2  Installation Procedure

  1. Swing cover into position.

  1. Insert top edge of side cover into inside of top cover. The distance between side cover rear edge and chassis should be about 20mm

  1. Move side cover forward and fix it on chassis

  1. Tighten the four screws at the side cover.

1.2   Top Cover

1.2.1  Removal Procedure

  1. Remove the two screws at behind of top cover. See Illustration 2

    Illustration 2:  Top Cover Removal

  1. Slide cover backward and lift it away.

1.2.2  Installation Procedure

  1. Insert two pins of top cover into the hole in the chassis.

  1. Slide cover forward and fix cover on chassis.

  1. Tighten two screws at behind of top cover

1.3   Front Cover

1.3.1  Removal Procedure

  1. From the service desktop, shut down the system.

  1. Turn off console power.

  1. Remove two screws at right side of console (two screws on each side). See Illustration 3

    Illustration 3:  Front Cover Removal

    .

  1. Rotate bottom of cover outward and upward until it may be lifted free of the console at the top.

1.3.2  Installation Procedure

  1. Place the front cover onto console.

  1. Tighten two screws at both right and left side of console.

1.4   Power Switch Adjust

If the console power switch cannot fit properly and could not function well, adjust the Console power switch bracket by adding or removing the adjustment plate of the power switch bracket as below illustration. See Illustration 4. Two additional adjustment plates are stick inside TIO console between ICOM and AC Outlet box.
