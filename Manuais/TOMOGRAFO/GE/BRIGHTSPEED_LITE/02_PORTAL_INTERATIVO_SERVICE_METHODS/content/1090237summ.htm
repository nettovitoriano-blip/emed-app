3  Procedure

3.1   Power-Off (Shut Down) the Console

  1. Select one of the following methods to Power OFF the Operator Console:

    1. If Applications are UP, click on the [Shut Down] button and select [Shutdown]. The Operator Console monitor will display a 'Power Down' message when it is acceptable to power OFF the Operator Console.

    1. If Applications are DOWN, open a Unix Shell. Type: halt.

  1. Power OFF the console at the front panel switch. (See Illustration 1.)

    Illustration 1:  Console Power Switch

  1. Perform prescribed Lockout/Tagout procedure. For added protection, disconnect the Twist-N-Lock Main Power Cable from the rear of the console.

3.2   Remove the Existing Fan

  1. Loosen four screws at rear of console. See Illustration 2

    Illustration 2:  Rear of Console

  1. Disconnect the Power to the Fan. Firmly grasp the power connector with one hand and pull power connector from fan. (See illustration below.)

  1. Remove the four (4) 4mm socket cap screws, nuts, and washers that hold the Fan to the mounting bracket. Save the screws and the fan guard.

  1. Remove Fan and set aside.

    (Step Details)

3.3   Install Replacement Fan

  1. Install the replacement fan using the four (4) screws, nuts and fan guard from the previous step.

  1. Reattach the power to the fan.

3.4   Test the Replacement Fan

  1. Apply power to the console.

  1. Verify that the fan is blowing air in the proper direction.

    (Step Details)

4  Finalization

  1. Install the console Rear cover.

  1. Turn the system ON.
