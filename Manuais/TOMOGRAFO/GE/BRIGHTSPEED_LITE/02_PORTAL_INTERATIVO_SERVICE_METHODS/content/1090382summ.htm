HP xw8600 Host Computer Power Supply Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

CAUTION
Potential for injury.
The HP Computer module weighs approximately 25.2kg (55.6 lbs).
Use care, when removing the HP module.

NOTICE
To prevent damage to components within the HP xw8x00 chassis, observe the following ESD precautions:

  • Work on a static-free mat.

  • Wear a static strap to ensure that any accumulated electrostatic charge is discharged from your body to ground.

  • Create a common ground for the equipment you are working on by connecting the static-free mat, static strap and peripheral units to that piece of equipment.

 

1  Procedure

1.1   Power OFF (Shut Down) the Console

  1. If Applications are up, click on the [Shut Down] button.

  1. Power OFF the Operator Console front switch, when instructed to “Power Down”.

  1. Remove left side console cover.

  1. Remove console rear panel to access host computer rear panel.

  1. Disconnect cables from host computer rear panel.

  1. Remove and retain the four (4) front mounting screws.

  1. Slide out the HP Host Computer module.

1.2   Remove Old Power Supply

  1. Remove the side access panel.

  1. Write down the numbers on the cables to easily reconnect the cables to the correct devices.

  1. Disconnect power supply cables from the system board, drives, and cards.

  1. Remove the four screws from the host computer back panel. Refer to Illustration 1

    Illustration 1:  Remove Power Supply

  1. Slide the power supply toward the front of the chassis, and then lift it out of the chassis.

1.3   Install New Power Supply

To replace the power supply, reverse the steps in the previous section.

1.4   Power connections to system components

For help with identifying power cables, see below illustration and Table . Ensure that all cables are routed or tied so they cannot interfere with the processor heatsink fans.
