3  Procedure

3.1   Power-Off (Shut Down) the Console

  1. Select one of the following methods to power off the Operator Console:

    (Step Details)

  1. Power OFF the Operator Console Switch at the front panel. (See Illustration 2.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform prescribed Lockout/Tagout procedure. For added protection, disconnect the Twist-N-Lock Main Power Cable from the rear of the console.

3.2   Remove Console Network Switch (CNS)

  1. Remove the side and top Operator Console Covers per prescribed cover removal procedure.

  1. Remove the Ethernet network cable connections to the CNS being replaced.

    (Step Details)

  1. To allow the removal of the CNS from the console, cut of the two belts of the Ethernet Switch.

  1. Remove Ethernet Switch from top of console.

3.3   Install the Replacement CNS

  1. Slide the replacement CNS into the Operator Console from the front.

  1. Reinstall the two belts at both side of Ethernet Switch and verify it can be fixed the Switch.

  1. Mount the power cord to the rear of the console.

  1. Reinstall the Ethernet cable connection(s) to their original locations according to all cables are labeled and clearly marked.

    (Step Details)

3.4   Power-On the Operator Console

  1. Remove Lockout Tagout protection applied earlier.

  1. Power ON the Operator Console Switch at the console front panel.

3.5   Verify Replacement CNS Operation

  1. Visually verify the CNS front panel Power LED is illuminated.

  1. Visually verify that all appropriate Port LEDs show active links. The CPU LED should be blinking during normal operation.

4  Finalization

  1. Refer to System Scanning Test to confirm proper operation.

  1. Reinstall Console Side and Top Covers.
