ICOM Fuse Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT
USE PROPER LOCKOUT / TAGOUT PROCEDURES BEFORE REPLACING THE COMPONENTS.

NOTICE
FOLLOW ALL REQUIRED SAFETY AND PPE PROCEDURES CUSTOMARY FOR YOUR ORGANIZATION WHEN WORKING ON THIS PRODUCT.
 

1  Procedure

1.1   Power OFF (Shut Down) the Console

  1. If Applications are up, click on the [Shut Down] button.

  1. Power OFF the Operator Console front switch, when instructed to “Power Down”.

1.2   Remove Old Fuse

  1. Remove right side console cover.

  1. Switch off ICOM at the rear side of ICOM. See Illustration 1

    Illustration 1:  Fuse Location

  1. Push out the Fuse Drawer by using a small flat screwdriver. See Illustration 2

    Illustration 2:  Fuse Drawer

  1. Identify and remove the defective Fuse(s).

1.3   Install New Fuse

  1. Install the new Fuse (8A 250V Time Delay Fuse) into the Fuse Drawer

  1. Switch on ICOM at the rear side of ICOM.

  1. Restore the console cover.

2  Finalization

  1. Take some scans which need to do prescribed tilt.

  1. Verify the prescribed tilt function is normal.
