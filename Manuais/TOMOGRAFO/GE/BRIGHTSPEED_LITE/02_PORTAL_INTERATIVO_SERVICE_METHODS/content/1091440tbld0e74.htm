Workstation powered off automatically and the Power LED flashes red two times, once every second, followed by a two-second pause.Processor thermal protection activated

A Fan might blocked or not turning

OR

The processor heatsink fan assembly is not properly attached to the processor
  1. Ensure that the workstation air vents are not blocked.
  2. Open the access panel, press the workstation power button.
  3. Verify that the system fan is running.
  4. Verify that the processor heatsink fan spins. if the heatsink fan is not spinning, verify that the fan cable is plugged into the system board connector.Verify that the fan is properly seated.
  5. Replace the processor heatsink.
Power LED flashes red, once every two seconds.Power failure (power supply overloaded)
  1. Determine whether a device is causing the problem by performing the follows:
    1. Disconnect AC power.
    2. Remove all attached devices.
    3. Power on the workstation.
    If the system enters the POST, perform the following:
    1. Power off the workstation.
    2. Replace one device at a time and repeat this procedure until a failure occurs.
    3. Replace the device causing the failure.
    4. Continue adding devices one at a time to verify that all devices are functioning.
  2. Verify power supply functionality as follows:
    1. Disconnect AC power.
    2. Remove all internal power supply cables from the system board.
    3. Plug in AC power.
      • If the power supply fan spins and the LED illuminates, the power supply is good. Replace the system board.
      • If the power supply fan does not spin or the LED does not illuminate, replace the power supply.
