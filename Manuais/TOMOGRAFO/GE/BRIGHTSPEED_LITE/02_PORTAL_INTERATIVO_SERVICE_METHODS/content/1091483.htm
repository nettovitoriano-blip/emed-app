4  Procedure

4.1  Test Procedure

  1. If GPU card is not installed, check monitors function. If monitors display normally, Graphics Card FX1700 works normally, no test is needed.

  1. If GPU card is installed, perform following checks:

    1. {ctuser@hostname} [2] su -

    1. Password:enter

    1. {root@hostname} # gpudiag

  1. Take some scans, verify the image(s) reconstruct and are displayed.
