3  Procedure

3.1   Preparation for Replacement

NOTICE
This procedure uses many screw, lock and flat washer combinations. Make sure the order of installation has the lock washer positioned between the flat washer and head of the screw.
 

  1. Remove gantry right side cover and disable Axial drive and HVDC service switches from the service switch panel.

    (Step Details)

  1. Position the tube at 4 o'clock position for easy access to brake assembly.

  1. Disable the 120 VAC service switch.

  1. Remove the gantry left side, top and front covers. Connect the cover E-stop circuit to the terminators on the gantry.

  1. Disconnect thermostat cable from connection on back side of resistor assembly. Refer to Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Loosen cable clamp and disconnect brake resistor cable from X3 port of axial drive controller. Refer to Illustration 2. Loosen and/or remove the side safety cover if needed for extra space when removing cable.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove existing brake resistor assembly from axial drive assembly using a 5mm hex wrench. Refer to Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

3.2   Installation of Brake Resistor Assembly on Assembly Frame, Cabling and Routing

  1. Mount the resistor assembly to side of axial drive box with two (2) M6 screws, lock washers and flat washers using a 5mm hex wrench. Refer to Illustration 2and torque according to the table below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect thermostat cable to connection on back side of resistor assembly. Refer to Illustration 1.

  1. Route resistor cable the same way it was routed on the previous assembly and connect into the X3 port of the axial drive. Refer to Illustration 2. Tighten cable clamp around cable. Loosen and/or remove the side safety cover if needed for extra space when installing cable.

3.3   Reassembly of Gantry

  1. Fasten the side safety cover on the tilting assembly if loosened or removed during previous steps.

  1. Install the gantry front cover, top and left side covers.

  1. Turn on the Axial drive, HVDC and 120VAC service switches from the service switch panel.

  1. Install the gantry right side cover.

4  Finalization

  1. Run the System Scanning Test from the Functional Checks procedure list.
