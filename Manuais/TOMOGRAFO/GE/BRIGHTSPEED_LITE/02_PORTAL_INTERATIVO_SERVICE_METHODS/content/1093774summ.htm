3  Procedure

3.1   Preparation for Replacement

NOTICE
This procedure uses many screw, lock washer and flat washer combinations. Make sure the order of installation has the lock washer positioned between the flat washer and head of the screw.
 

  1. Remove gantry right side cover and disable Axial drive, HVDC and 120VAC service switches from the service switch panel.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the gantry left side, top and front covers. Connect the cover E-stop circuit to the terminators on the gantry.

  1. Disconnect KIM com and TGPU cables from KIM board. Refer to Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect axial drive cable and power supply cable from KIM board. Refer to Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove Velcro strap holding wires in place at the back of the assembly. This strap is connected to the KIM bracket, which will be removed with removal of the KIM assembly in the next step.

  1. Remove existing KIM board assembly from axial drive assembly using a 3mm hex wrench. Refer to Illustration 3. Removing the entire assembly allows for easier access when replacing the KIM board itself.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the plastic cover from the KIM board bracket by flexing the plastic. Keep the cover to replace it after the new KIM board is in place.

  1. Remove the four (4) M3 screws and small screws on the board to remove the KIM board from the bracket. Refer to Illustration 4. Keep the screws to use in the replacement of the new KIM board.

3.2   Installing KIM Board on Assembly Frame

  1. Mount the new KIM board to bracket with four (4) M3 screws and flat washers with a 2.5mm hex wrench. Refer to Illustration 4.

  1. Use small screws from old KIM assembly to attach new assembly to bracket.

    (Step Details)

  1. Mount bracket onto axial drive assembly with two (2) M4 screws, lock washers and flat washers using a 3mm hex wrench. Refer to Illustration 3.

  1. Mount plastic cover to top of assembly, flexing cover to fit in bracket slots. Refer to Illustration 5 for any questions regarding mounting procedure.

    (Step Details)

3.3   Cable Installation and Routing

  1. Connect the wire from the X1 port of the axial drive to the larger port on the side of the KIM board. Refer to Illustration 2.

  1. Connect red and black cables from the power supply into the smaller KIM board port. Refer to Illustration 2.

  1. Connect the KIM com cable into the KIM board assembly. Refer to Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the TGPU cable into the top back side of the KIM board assembly. Refer to Illustration 1.

3.4   Reassemble gantry

  1. Install the gantry front cover, top and left side covers.

  1. Turn on the Axial drive, HVDC and 120VAC services switches from the service switch panel.

  1. Install the gantry right side cover.

4  Finalization

  1. Run the System Scanning Test from the Functional Checks procedure list.

  1. Plug in any options the customer has to the interface panels as applicable for your site. Perform any defined functional checks for the options plugged into the interface panels.
