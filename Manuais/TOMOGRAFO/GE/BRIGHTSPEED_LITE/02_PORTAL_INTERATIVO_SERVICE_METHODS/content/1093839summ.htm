3  Procedure

3.1   Preparation for Replacement

NOTICE
This procedure uses many screw, lock and flat washer combinations. Make sure the order of installation has the lock washer positioned between the flat washer and head of the screw.
 

  1. Remove gantry right side cover and disable Axial Drive, HVDC and 120VAC service switches from the Service Switch Panel. Refer to Replacement > Gantry > Enclosure > Cover Removal Procedures for removal and replacement of covers.

  1. Remove the gantry left side, top and front covers. Connect the cover E-stop circuit to the terminators on the gantry.

  1. Remove the plastic shield and disconnect red and black cable from the smaller port on the KIM board assembly (refer to Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect power supply (blue and brown) wire from 2010 axial drive (refer to Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Pull the cables out of the cable clamps for the cables running to the power supply. These are supplied with the new part.

  1. Remove the three screws mounting the power supply assembly to the axial drive assembly. The bottom mounting screw is accessible from the outside of the axial drive assembly. See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. To remove the power supply, pull supply assembly up and out of the top of the chassis.

3.2   Installation of new Power Supply Assembly

  1. Mount power supply assembly onto axial drive assembly with three M6 screws, lock washers and flat washers using a 5mm hex wrench. Refer to Illustration 3and table below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect red and black cable from the power supply into the KIM board port and reinstall plastic shield over KIM board. See Illustration 1. Guide cable through the two plastic cable clamps on the route between the power supply and KIM board. See Illustration 4 for visual reference of plastic cable clamps.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect other cable to the power supply wire (blue and brown) from the 2010 axial drive. See Illustration 2. Guide wire through plastic cable clamp near the power supply (see Illustration 4).

3.3   Reassembly of Gantry

  1. Turn on the 120VAC switch from the Service Switch Panel. An 'o2' code should show up on the display of the axial drive controller (refer to Illustration 5)

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn off 120VAC switch from the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Install the gantry front cover, then the top and left side covers.

  1. Turn on the 120VAC, Axial Drive, and HVDC service switches from the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Install the gantry right side cover.

4  Finalization

  1. Run System Scanning Test from the Functional Checks procedure list.
