PDAS Air Plenum Cooling Fans Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

DANGER
CRUSH HAZARD.
UNEXPECTED GANTRY ROTATION CAN CAUSE SERIOUS INJURY.
Never service the gantry with the axial drive enabled. Use the gantry rotation lock to lock out gantry rotation. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

NOTICE
Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization when working on this product.
 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

3  Procedure

3.1   Cooling Fan Removal

  1. Move the table cradle to the home position, and position the table to its lowest elevation.

  1. Remove gantry right side cover.

NOTICE
Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120VAC. Turning OFF 120VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 

  1. Turn OFF Axial Enable, HVDC, and 120VAC switches on the service switch panel.

  1. Remove the rest of the gantry covers.

  1. Position the DAS at 12 o’clock position.

  1. Lock the gantry in position using the rotational lock.

  1. Disconnect the fan power cable from the PDAS Duck Fan Cover.

  1. Remove the PDAS Duck Fan Cover by unscrewing its M6x12 allen screws by a on the PDAS Duck Fan Cover.

    Illustration 1:  Remove the PDAS Duck Fan ASM

  1. Disconnect the cooling fan power cable connector from the DAS Duck Fan ASM.

    Illustration 2:  ADB Cooling Fan Removal

  1. Remove the cooling fan by unscrewing its four screws.

3.2   Cooling Fan Installation

  1. Get new cooling fan and secure by four M4 screws. (Torque each screw: 2.3Nm)

  1. Reconnect PS cable from DAS cover to fans.

  1. Reinstall the fan cover by first engaging all M6 screws (torque is 7.9Nm).

  1. Connect the fan power cable from the PDAS Duck Fan Cover.

  1. Disengage the rotational lock.

  1. Turn on Axial Enable, HVDC, and 120VAC switches.
