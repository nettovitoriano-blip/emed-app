4  Procedure

  1. Move the table to the lowest elevation.

WARNING
Potential for Shock.
Voltage may be present.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures.

  1. Turn off the facility power to PDU.

  1. Remove the Gantry side covers.

  1. Lift off the top cover.

  1. Remove the "scan" window.

  1. Remove the front cover.

  1. Clean or replace Pancake Das Fan Filter

    1. Rotate the Gantry until the DAS/DUCT Fan assembly is within serviceable reach.

    1. Engage the rotational lock. See Illustration 1

      Illustration 1:  Rotational Lock

    1. Disconnect the fan harness.

    NOTICE
    POTENTIAL FOR EQUIPMENT DAMAGE.

    USING VACUUM HOSE NEAR LIVE ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT MAY CAUSE EQUIPMENT DAMAGE DUE TO STATIC.

    MAKE SURE EQUIPMENT IS TURNED OFF PRIOR TO VACUUMING NEAR ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT.

     

    1. Vacuum the dust from fans on the DAS fan cover

    1. Remove the fan cover from DAS chassis. See Illustration 2

    1. Vacuum the dust from the filter, See Illustration 3 or replace the filter if necessary, replacement procedure refer to PDAS ADB Filter Replacement

      Illustration 2:  DAS Fan Cover Removal

      Illustration 3:  DAS Filter

  1. Clean or replace DIFB Fan Filter

    1. Two DIFB Fans located behind the Gantry DAS frame, remove the DIFB fan filter by unscrewing its 4 screws on each fan. See Illustration 4

      Illustration 4:  DIFB Fan

    1. Vacuum the dust filter on the DIFB Fan, or replace the filter if necessary

    1. Restore the DIFB Fan Filter by screwing its 4 screws on each Fan.

  1. Restore the fan harness connection.

  1. Reassemble the Gantry covers and the scan window.

  1. Restore the power.
