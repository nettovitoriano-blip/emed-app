Pancake DAS 16 General Cleaning

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

NOTICE
Failure in properly using the Aero Duster Spray System and ESD grounding generates a static charge of DAS components up to 12,000 volts.
 

  1. Accumulate cleaning materials.

  1. Take a DC Noise baseline scan using DASTOOLS Manual test.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn Off DAS Power Supplies.

  1. Remove the Air Plenum.

  1. Remove Pancake DAS covers. Follow the procedure of Pancake DAS covers removal.

  1. Manually rotate the Gantry so that the DAS is at the 90 degree position.

  1. Remove all ADB cards from the backplanes in sequence.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
When using Aero Duster and the Aero Duster Spray System, do not invert the can as this will cause liquid to come out. Testing has shown that this can cause a large static charge to develop where the spray hits.

  1. Perform Image Quality testing.
