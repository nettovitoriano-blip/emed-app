Fan Harness Assembly Replacement

  Safety

DANGER
ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT
USE PROPER LOCKOUT / TAGOUT PROCEDURES BEFORE REPLACING THE COMPONENTS.

NOTICE
FOLLOW ALL REQUIRED SAFETY AND PPE PROCEDURES CUSTOMARY FOR YOUR ORGANIZATION WHEN WORKING ON THIS PRODUCT.
 

1  Procedure

1.1   Removal Procedure

  1. Select one of the following methods to Power OFF the Operator Console:

    1. If Applications are up, click on the [Shut Down] button and select [Shutdown].

      (Step Details)

    1. If Applications are down, open a Unix Shell. Type: halt.

  1. Power OFF the console at the front panel switch.

  1. Remove the top cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove four screws on the top panel of AC Box Assembly, and remove the top panel.

    Illustration 1:  Top Panel of AC Box Assembly Removal

  1. Disconnect a power cable and a GND cable form fan harness.

    Illustration 2:  Power Cable and GND Cable

  1. Remove four screws that hold the fan to the AC box Assembly, and remove the fan.

    Illustration 3:  Fan Removal

1.2   Installation Procedure

  1. Install a new fan using the four screws and fan guard.

  1. Re-Connect the power cable and GND cable to the fan.

  1. Power ON the Operator Console at the console front panel switch.

  1. Verify that the fan is blowing air in the proper direction.

2  Finalization

  1. Power OFF the console at the front panel switch.

  1. Re-Install the top panel of the AC box assembly.

  1. Re-Install the console cover.
