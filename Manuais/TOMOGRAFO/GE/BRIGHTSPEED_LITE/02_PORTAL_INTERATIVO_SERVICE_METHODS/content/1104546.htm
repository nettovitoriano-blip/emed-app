4  Procedure

4.1  Power-Off (Shut Down) the Console

System requires a power-down sequence when replacing the DVD Peripheral Tower. Operating System may not recognize the USB port connections if the DVD Peripheral Tower is replaced without cycling the console power.

  1. Select one of the following methods to power off the Operator Console:

    • If applications are running, click the Shut Down icon and select Shut Down.

    • If applications are down, open a Unix Shell using the Toolchest. Type: {ctuser@hostname} halt. Press <Enter>.

    The Operator Console monitor will display a System Halted message when it is acceptable to power off the Operator Console.

  1. Power OFF the Operator Console at the front panel switch.

4.2  Remove Old DVD Peripheral Tower

  1. Remove the USB cable connections from the back of the peripheral tower chassis being replaced.

    NOTE:Verify that all cables are labeled and clearly marked; if necessary, add a label for clarity.

  1. Remove the power cord(s) at the rear of the DVD Peripheral Tower chassis.

  1. Remove the DVD Peripheral Tower and set aside.

4.3  Install Replacement DVD Peripheral Tower

  1. Place the replacement DVD Peripheral Tower chassis on the Operator Console.

  1. Mount the power cord(s) to the rear of the DVD Peripheral Tower chassis. Verify that the power switch on the DVD Peripheral Tower power supply is turned to the ON position.

  1. Replace the rear USB cable connections.

    Illustration 2:  DVD Peripheral Tower Connections

    NOTE:It is important that the USB Cables be connected to their assigned ports on the Host Computer and the DVD Peripheral Drive Tower to maintain proper drive assignment (see RIO Console Schematic 5577708SCH).

4.4  Verify Replacement DVD Peripheral Tower Operation

  1. Power ON the Operator Console at the console front panel switch. Visually verify the Peripheral Tower front panel Power LED is illuminated and that each of the DVD Drive Access LEDs illuminates when media is inserted and the tray closed.

  1. Verify that the cooling fan at the rear of the DVD Peripheral Tower is operational.

  1. No other verifications required.

5  Finalization

Refer to Peripheral Tower Functional Test to confirm proper operation.
