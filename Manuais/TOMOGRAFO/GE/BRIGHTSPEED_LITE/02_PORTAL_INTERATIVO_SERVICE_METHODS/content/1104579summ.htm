FOLLOW ALL REQUIRED SAFETY AND PPE PROCEDURES CUSTOMARY FOR YOUR ORGANIZATION WHEN WORKING ON THIS PRODUCT.
 

1  Procedure

1.1   Power-Off (Shut Down) the Console

  1. Select one of the following methods to power off the Operator Console:

    (Step Details)

  1. Power OFF the Operator Console at the front panel switch.

  1. Perform prescribed Lockout/Tagout procedure. For added protection, disconnect the Twist-N-Lock Main Power Cable from rear of console.

1.2   Remove the Old Keyboard, Mouse, Trackball, Barcode Reader or PMT

  1. Remove the USB cable connection from USB junction cable connector, for the Keyboard, Mouse, Trackball, Barcode Reader or PMT being replaced, and remove the accessory (see Illustration 1).

    Illustration 1:  USB Cable Connection

1.3   Install the Replacement Keyboard, Mouse, Trackball, Barcode Reader or PMT

  1. Place the replacement Keyboard, Mouse, Trackball or PMT on the Operator Console.

  1. Plug in Keyboard, Mouse, Trackball or Barcode Reader cable in the USB connector at USB junction cable connector.

1.4   Power-On the Operator Console

  1. Power ON the Operator Console at the console front panel switch.

1.5   Verify Replacement Keyboard, Mouse, Trackball, Barcode Reader or PMT Operation

  1. Visually verify Keyboard, Mouse, Trackball, Barcode Reader or PMT works as expected with the CT Applications.

  1. No other verifications required.
