2  Overview

This procedure explains how to remove the DVD RAM cartridge for option key DVD installation to new PMT (5270510-10).

Last revised:February 25, 2011

3  Procedure

3.1  In case of Maxell

  1. Remove the 2 pins as shown in the following picture.

    Illustration 1:  Locking Pins

  1. Put the stick (like ball point pen) and pull out the cartridge flap.

    Illustration 2:  Cartridge Flap

  1. Remove the DVD media.

    Illustration 3:  DVD Media

  1. Insert the media into the original cartridge for identification after usage of option installation.

    Illustration 4:  Media Inserting

  1. Close and lock the flap, keep the media in a cabinet.

    Illustration 5:  Flap Closing

3.2  In case of Panasonic or Verbatim

  1. Remove the 2 pins as shown in the following picture.

    Illustration 6:  Locking Pins

  1. Push the stick at right side of DVD RAM (with A side upwards) and swing out the cartridge flap.

    Illustration 7:  Stick at the Right Side

  1. Remove the DVD media.

    Illustration 8:  DVD Media

  1. Insert the media into the original cartridge for identification after usage of option installation.

  1. Close and lock the flap.

    Illustration 9:  Flap Closing
