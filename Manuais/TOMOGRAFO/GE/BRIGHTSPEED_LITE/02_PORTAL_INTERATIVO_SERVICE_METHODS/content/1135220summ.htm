3  Procedure

3.1   Removal Procedure

  1. Select one of the following methods to Power OFF the Operator Console:

    1. If Applications are up, click on the [Shut Down] button and select [Shutdown].

      (Step Details)

    1. If Applications are down, open a Unix Shell. Type: halt.

  1. Power OFF the console at the front panel switch.

  1. Remove the top and front covers.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove four screws that hold the power switch assembly to the console chassis.

    Illustration 1:  Four Screws Removal

  1. Disconnect all cables from back of the power switch assembly, and remove the power switch assembly.

3.2   Installation Procedure

  1. Reconnect all cables to a new power switch assembly.

  1. Install the new power switch assembly using the four screws.

  1. Power ON the Operator Console Switch at the console front panel.

  1. Verify that power is restored to all the console components.

  1. Remove console power.

4  Finalization

  1. Re-Install the top and front cover.
