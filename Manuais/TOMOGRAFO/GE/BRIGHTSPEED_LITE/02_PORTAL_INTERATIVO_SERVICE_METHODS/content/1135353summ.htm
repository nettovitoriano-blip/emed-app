3  Procedure

3.1   Removal Procedure

  1. Select one of the following methods to Power OFF the Operator Console:

    1. If Applications are up, click on the [Shut Down] button and select [Shutdown].

      (Step Details)

    1. If Applications are down, open a Unix Shell. Type: halt.

  1. Power OFF the console at the front panel switch.

    Illustration 1:  Power Switch

  1. Remove the top cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect all cable connectors from the AC Box Assembly.

    Illustration 2:  Cable Connectors of AC Box Assembly

  1. Remove six screws from AC Box as below illustration, and remove the AC box assembly.

3.2   Installation Procedure

  1. Install the new AC box assembly using 6 screws.

  1. Re-Connect all cable connectors to the AC box assembly.

4  Finalization

  1. Re-Install the console cover.
