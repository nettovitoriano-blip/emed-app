3.3  Safety

NOTICE
TO PREVENT DAMAGE TO COMPONENTS WITHIN THE Z800, OBSERVE THE FOLLOWING ESD PRECAUTIONS:

  • WORK ON A STATIC-FREE MAT.

  • WEAR A STATIC STRAP TO ENSURE THAT ANY ACCUMULATED ELECTROSTATIC CHARGE IS DISCHARGED FROM YOUR BODY TO GROUND.

  • CREATE A COMMON GROUND FOR THE EQUIPMENT YOU ARE WORKING ON BY CONNECTING THE STATIC-FREE MAT, STATIC STRAP AND PERIPHERAL UNITS TO THAT PIECE OF EQUIPMENT.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION REGARDING ESD PROTECTION, REFER TO THE SAFETY CHAPTER IN THIS PUBLICATION

 

NOTICE
IN ORDER TO MEET EMC REQUIREMENTS, THE CHASSIS MODULES MUST BE PROPERLY INSTALLED AFTER SERVICING. CHECK THAT THE MODULES ARE MOUNTED FIRMLY IN PLACE AND LOCKED DOWN WHERE APPLICABLE.
 

NOTICE
FOLLOW ALL REQUIRED SAFETY AND PPE PROCEDURES CUSTOMARY FOR YOUR ORGANIZATION WHEN WORKING ON THIS PRODUCT.

4  Procedure

  1. Select one of the following methods to Power OFF the Operator Console:

    1. If Applications are up, click on the [Shut Down] button and select [Shutdown].

      The Operator Console monitor will display a 'Power Down' message when it is acceptable to power OFF the Operator Console.

    1. If Applications are down, open a Unix Shell. Type: halt.

  1. Power OFF the console at the front panel switch.

  1. Remove the front and left side covers from the console.

    Refer to Replacement → Console → (Console Cover Removal and Installation Procedure).

  1. Remove the side access panel from the host computer (Z800).

  1. Replace the existing scanfile hard disk drive (HDD) with new one.

    Illustration 1:  Scanfile Hard Disk Drive

  1. Reinstall the side access panel.

5  Finalization

  1. Power ON the operator console. Start up sequence stops before application start up.

  1. Run “Reconfig”, select “System”, select “Yes” under the “Regenerate Scan Database” and press “Accept”.

  1. System reboots automatically and stops before application.

  1. Open shell window and type st to start up application.

  1. Refer to System Scanning Test to confirm proper operation.

  1. Reinstall the console covers.
