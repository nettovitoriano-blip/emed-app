1.1   IVY 7800 Cardiac Monitor Installation

Contents of the Cardiac Monitor collector are shown in Table 1.

Table 1:  Major Parts

ItemP/NQtyDescription
154612651IVY 7800 ECG with GE Interface and Patient Impedance Detection
253140851CABLE ASM, GANTRY - EKG SIGNAL
35193969-51Cable - LAN
453139431Accessory Ground Cable, Cardiac, Kitty Hawk
553139441Cable - Power, Monitor to Gantry

  1. Setup/assemble the Cardiac Monitor cart. (Refer to assembly manual.)

  1. Attach the Cardiac Monitor to the cart.

  1. Refer to Illustration 1 for the remaining steps in this section.

    (Step Details)

  1. Verify Voltage Line Select switch is set to 115V.

  1. (For systems with IPC only) Attach Ground cable to Ground Lug at rear of Cardiac Monitor.

  1. (For systems with IPC only) Attach Ground cable to Ground Lug on the IPC Panel.

  1. Connect AC (IEC) power cable to the back of the Cardiac Monitor.

  1. Connect the AC (IEC) power cable to the Power connector (IEC) at the Gantry Options Panel (IPC).

  1. Connect signal cable 5193969-5 and 5314085 according to Table 2.

    (Step Details)

1.2   Enabling the IVY Cardiac Monitor

  1. At the console select:

    1. Common Service Desktop.

    1. Configuration tab.

    1. [Install Gating Monitor].

  1. From the menu select:

    1. [IVY7800].

    1. [Accept].
