3.3  Safety

NOTICE
TO PREVENT DAMAGE TO COMPONENTS WITHIN THE Host Computer, OBSERVE THE FOLLOWING ESD PRECAUTIONS:

  • WORK ON A STATIC-FREE MAT.

  • WEAR A STATIC STRAP TO ENSURE THAT ANY ACCUMULATED ELECTROSTATIC CHARGE IS DISCHARGED FROM YOUR BODY TO GROUND.

  • CREATE A COMMON GROUND FOR THE EQUIPMENT YOU ARE WORKING ON BY CONNECTING THE STATIC-FREE MAT, STATIC STRAP AND PERIPHERAL UNITS TO THAT PIECE OF EQUIPMENT.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION REGARDING ESD PROTECTION, REFER TO THE SAFETY CHAPTER IN THIS PUBLICATION

 

NOTICE
IN ORDER TO MEET EMC REQUIREMENTS, THE CHASSIS MODULES MUST BE PROPERLY INSTALLED AFTER SERVICING. CHECK THAT THE MODULES ARE MOUNTED FIRMLY IN PLACE AND LOCKED DOWN WHERE APPLICABLE.
 

NOTICE
FOLLOW ALL REQUIRED SAFETY AND PPE PROCEDURES CUSTOMARY FOR YOUR ORGANIZATION WHEN WORKING ON THIS PRODUCT.

4  Procedure

  1. Save all the BIOS customized configuration to removal media.

    NOTE:For xw8400/xw8600/z400/z800 can use the USB device to save and restore the BIOS customized configuration, please refer to Troubleshooting → Console → HP BIOS Customized Configuration Save and Restore Procedure with USB device to save and restore the BIOS customized configuration.

    For xw8200 can’t use the USB device to save and restore BIOS customized configuration, please follow the Load GE Required BIOS Configuration Software in PC BIOS Install & Setup for HP xw8200 Host Computer to save and restore the customized configuration.

    For xw8000 also can’t use the USB device to save and restore the BIOS customized configuration, please follow the Host Computer BIOS Standard Setting Procedure in ADVANCED DIAGNOSTICS manual to restore the BIOS customized configuration.

  1. Select one of the following methods to Power OFF the Operator Console:

    1. If Applications are up, click on the [Shut Down] button and select [Shutdown].

      The Operator Console monitor will display a 'Power Down' message when it is acceptable to power OFF the Operator Console.

    1. If Applications are down, open a Unix Shell. Type: halt.

  1. Power OFF the console at the front panel switch when ‘System Halted’ message is displayed, remove the power supply from the host computer (disconnect the power cord if connected).

  1. Press and hold PC power switch for at least 10 seconds after removing power cord. This will help to drain the stored power of the PC power supply unit.

  1. Remove the console cover and host computer cover refer to the relevant procedure.

  1. Remove the defective battery from the mainboard.

    NOTE: If the battery is covered, need to remove the relevant components priority from the mainboard.

  1. Let the system sit for 1~2 minutes.

  1. Install the new battery, then restore the power and restart the host computer.

  1. Press [F10] as soon as the display is active and the HP logo displayed on the screen.

  1. Restore the BIOS customized configuration.

  1. Reinstall the console covers.
