3  Procedure

3.1   Schleifring Slip Ring PM Requirements

  1. Vacuum brush dust at the following intervals:

    1. The FIRST 250k gantry revolutions after brush module replacement.

    1. Every 500k gantry revolutions thereafter.

    1. It is required to inspect the slip ring and brushes while vacuuming.

3.2   General Information about Slip Ring Vacuuming

  1. The vacuum brush must be free of contaminates and oil. Do not introduce foreign contaminates from a dirty or oily brush.

  1. Regardless of how heavy or old the brush dust accumulation is, a good vacuuming to remove ALL of the dust is sufficient for Schleifring maintenance. Do not use solvents to remove brush dust.

  1. When done properly and on a regular PM schedule, slip ring vacuuming is ample to avoid pre-mature power ring, brush or communication failures. It will also prevent any need for ring repair, or craytex sanding.

  1. Not removing ALL of the brush dust will cause arcing on the power rings. This will necessitate repair action that involves cratex sanding and use of alcohol for cratex residue removal. This is not a good thing because:

    1. Alcohol, as a binding agent, will encourage dust accumulation in ring crevices and will contaminate the brush tips, resulting in increased communication issues, maintenance and other problems.

    1. It is never necessary to use alcohol on the Schleifring during normal operation and wear of this ring. Alcohol cleaning is a service action to repair damage resulting from improper vacuuming; it is NOT a PM function.

  1. Arcing on the power rings will most times cause pitting and will necessitate sanding with the Craytex sanding crayon. Sanding the rings will remove ring material and significantly reduce ring life and if the residue from the sanding is not completely removed, will significantly increase brush wear. Sanding with a Craytex sanding crayon is a service repair action resulting from improper vacuuming, it is NOT a PM function.

  1. Remove the grounding strap from the power brush block.

  1. During the vacuuming process, visually inspect all slip ring tracks. Properly seasoned tracks will normally have a brownish black lubricant laid on the ring. This “patina” of carbon / silver is two to three thousands thick, and self-renewing. DO NOT remove this film of lubricant.

3.4   VACUUMING

  1. Vacuuming the Signal brushes.

    1. Remove all the brush blocks.

    1. Tap lightly with a small hammer to dislodge the dust particles.

    1. Vacuum thoroughly.

    1. Do not replace the removed components until the rest of the ring is vacuumed.

  1. Vacuuming the Power brushes (Same as Signal brush vacuuming).

    1. Remove the brush block.

    1. Tap lightly with a small hammer to dislodge the dust particles.

    1. Vacuum thoroughly.

    1. Do not replace the removed components until the rest of the ring is vacuumed.

  1. Check both top and bottom tips for significant wear. Expect the brush tips to wear at slightly different rates and they may appear out of alignment. DO NOT TOUCH THE TIPS WITH YOUR FINGER OR ANY TOOL.

  1. Vacuum the inside of the Slip Ring Cover.

  1. Vacuum the Gantry Rotating base.

  1. Vacuum the Slip Ring Itself. DO NOT TOUCH THE RING WITH YOUR FINGERS.

  1. Replace all of the removed components.

  1. Reassemble of the component is critical to brush and ring performance. Please refer to the proper replacement procedures in the General Service Manual.

  1. Install the slip ring covers.
