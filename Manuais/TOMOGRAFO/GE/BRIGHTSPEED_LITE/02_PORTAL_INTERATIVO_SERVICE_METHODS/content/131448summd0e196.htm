Look for pits or deposits, which are sometimes hard to see. Deposits can be silver or black from arching. If there is a suspect area on the ring, take the end of a CLEAN tie wrap and gently drag it across the suspect area. You should be able to feel a vibration in the tie wrap if a pit or deposit is present. If pits or deposits are found, refer to PM procedure CTPM-1306 (Clean Slip Ring Tracks).
