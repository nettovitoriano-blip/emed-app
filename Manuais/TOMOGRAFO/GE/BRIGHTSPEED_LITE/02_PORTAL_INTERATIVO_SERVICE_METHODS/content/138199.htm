CTPM-1602 (Check Scan Control Pushbuttons)

(Click Here for Summary Version)

1  Personnel Requirements

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization
1Not Applicable5 minsNot Applicable

2  Overview

 Procedure Effectivity:

Assembly:SYSTEM
Subassembly:Console
Purpose:Check Operation of Final Scan Abort Control Pushbuttons
Last Revised:November 1996

3  Procedure

  1. Queue up ten axial scans.

  1. Verify that the following are operational:

    • Start scan (and keypad lights).

    • Stop scan (and keypad lights).

    • Pause scan.

    • Advance to scan.

    • Stop table.

No finalization steps.
