MDAS/GDAS 16 DDIF Cleaning Procedure

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

CAUTION
Store Amax Cleaner at Site with MSDS document.
Do not store in the company car.
Use spray as directed in this procedure. Safety glasses and Nitrile gloves must be worn when using the spray.

NOTICE
Failure to follow correct procedures can result in bent or broken Interposer pins. This can result in intermittent artifacts, noise, or DAS Chassis/Detector replacement. never use screwdrivers, pocket knives etc, to service the DDIF.
 

NOTICE
When using Aero Duster, do not invert the can as this will cause liquid to come out. Testing has shown that this can cause a large static charge to develop where the liquid spray hits.
 

NOTICE
Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization when working on this product.

3  Procedure

  1. The following steps are to be performed only if:

    (Step Details)

  1. Take DC Noise and DC Offsets baseline scan using DASTOOLS Manual test (use default settings) to establish the current noise characteristics of DAS. Do not troubleshoot any noise failures until after the cleaning.

  1. Use the DASTools Viewlog to view the results of the baseline noise test to see the channels that violate the noise specification. The specifications are shown next to the actual data for the failed channels and shows the converter card number.

  1. Position the table to its lowest position.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove gantry right side cover, and turn OFF all three (3) service switches.

    (Step Details)

  1. Continue to open the gantry, by removing applicable gantry covers.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn off all three switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC).

  1. Lock the gantry in position, using the rotational lock.

    (Step Details)

  1. All protective ESD materials should be in place (i.e. wrist straps, Nitrile gloves, Amax, and Aero Duster; ionizing fan set up at far end of the patient table).

  1. Remove DAS Air Plenum.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
Never use a vacuum cleaner to clean the DDIF. Damage to the detector can result, requiring detector replacement.
 

  1. Use assembled Aero Duster spray and nozzle, blow off the dust. Pay particular attention to the spaces between the Flex connections.

  1. Use Ionizing fan to remove any built-up charges.

NOTICE
STOP!

Assemble system and test to see if issue is resolved.

The following steps should be performed only if the above steps failed to resolve the issue.

 

NOTICE
Use extreme care not to pull on the flex leads. Let the flex extraction tool do the work. Failure to be gentle can result in detector damage, requiring detector replacement.
 

  1. Using the Flex Extraction Tool, disconnect both A and B flexes from the DDIF. See Illustration 1 and Illustration 2. Refer to DAS 16 DDIF Tools for details on the Flex Extraction Tool.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Dust Shield after both flexes have been disconnected.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using the Interposer Removal Tool, remove both Interposers. Refer to DAS 16 DDIF Tools for details on the Interposer Removal Tool.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using the magnifying glass, inspect the Dust Shield Retainer Clip.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
Make sure you remove the interposers at a right angle to the backplane. Inspect the pins after removal to ensure no pins have been bent. Do not attempt to straighten bent pins. Replace the interposer if bent pins or more than 5 degrees of motion on loose pins is observed.
 

NOTICE
Do not spray Amax directly on the detector or flex leads. Amax contains chemicals that can dissolve adhesives used in the construction of the detector.
 

  1. Using Amax spray cleaner, spray away from the detector toward the DAS chassis.

    (Step Details)

  1. Use Amax spray cleaner to clean the interposers.

    (Step Details)

  1. Use the Ionizing Fan to remove any built-up charge in the area.

  1. Carefully install the interposers using your Nitrile glove protected fingers.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the Dust Shield.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the flex leads using your Nitrile glove protected fingers.

    (Step Details)

  1. Assemble the rest of the gantry.

  1. Restore power.

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform Image Quality testing.
