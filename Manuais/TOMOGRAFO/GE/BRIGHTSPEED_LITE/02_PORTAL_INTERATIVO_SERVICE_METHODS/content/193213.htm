4  Procedure

  1. Halt the system and power OFF console.

    NOTE:The Console must be shut down during this replacement. If the Console is not powered off, the Console BIOS will not recognize the new SCSI Tower.

  1. Remove the SCSI cable, terminator and power cables.

    (Refer to schematic in the rear cover for cabling details. Click on the PDF icon, , for a scalable PDF version of the schematic.)

  1. Reinstall the replacement SCSI tower, cables and terminator.

    NOTE:The switch setting on the rear of the SCSI tower are not used.

  1. Turn ON the power to the Console and SCSI tower.

    Check that the green power LED on the SCSI terminator is “ON”. If not, check that the front SCSI tower switch is “ON.” Check that the terminator is seated and the power cable is connected.

  1. As the console boots, you will see the SCSI tower devices info appear in the SCSI inventory as the console boots.

    Example Output:

    Device 0 00 Direct-Access SEAGATE ST336753LW FW Rev: HPS2  (Host 36 GB operating sys drive)
Device 1 01 Direct-Access SEAGATE ST373453LW FW Rev: HPS2  (Host 73 GB Image Storage Drive#1)
Device 1 02 Direct-Access SEAGATE ST373453LW FW Rev: HPS2  (Host 73 GB Image Storage Drive#2)
Device 2 03 Optical Device SONY SMO-F551-SD FW Rev: 1.04  (Sony MOD)
Device 2 04 CD-ROM MATSHITA DVD-RAM LF-D521 FW Rev: A109  (SCSI Tower DVD-RAM)

    FW Rev: E372 If this information does not appear, recheck Step 4.

  1. Perform a Reconfig.

  1. At application level complete a save and restore for the MOD. This operation should occur without errors.

  1. Read the customer option DVD, HiSpeed CT/i Navigator Tip Tutorial CD-ROM - H4.2M4 (Linux/Pegasus/GOC3), p/n 2394901 - as a test of the DVD drive. The drive should operate without errors.

  1. Restore system for customer operation.
