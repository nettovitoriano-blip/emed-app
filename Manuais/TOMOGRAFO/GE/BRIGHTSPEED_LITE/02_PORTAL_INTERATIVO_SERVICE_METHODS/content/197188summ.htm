CTPM-2010 (GOC3 Console Inspection)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Task Overview

  1. Vacuum the following:

    1. Console Fans and Air Intake Screen (Filters if present)

    1. Modules Fans and Air Intake Screens

    1. Console Interior

    1. SCSI Tower Fan and Air Intake Screens

  1. Clean the following components:

    1. Monitors - Clean monitor face and check for burnt screen

    1. Keyboard - Clean keys and using Aero Duster clean debris from keyboard

    1. SCIM - Clean push buttons, and using Aero Duster clean debris from SCIM

    1. Trackball Clean switches and using Aero Duster clean debris from trackball

    1. Mouse - Replace as needed

  1. On the Cabinet and the Desktop surface:

    1. Perform a visual inspection

    1. Check EMC gaskets

    1. Clean all fans

    1. Check for loose and worn cables

  1. Operational Functional Check:

    1. MOD & DVD

    1. Mouse, Keyboard, SCIM & Trackball

1.2   Fan Visual Operation Check (Power On Check)

  1. Check that the fan in the rear of the SCSI tower is operational.

  1. Remove console front cover and check that console back chassis fans are operational. (Power On Check.)

  1. Check that the fan in the rear of the computer is operational.

1.3   System Power-Down

  1. Turn OFF Power on front of console.

  1. Turn OFF power panel breaker.

  1. LOCK OUT / TAG OUT!

1.4   General Console Cleaning & Inspection

  1. Using a vacuum cleaner with HEPA filter and soft brush, carefully clean the interior, inlets and fan exhaust ports. Verify that the console is clean and clear of dirt and obstructions.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using cleaning materials supplied in the install support kit, perform the following actions:

    1. Use Windex and a damp lint free towel to clean the monitor screen and SCSI Tower front bezel.

    1. Use 409 cleaner and a damp lint free towel to clean the keyboard, SCIM, trackball, and desktop.

    1. Use an Aero Duster or can compressed air to remove debris from the keyboard trackball. Replace any broken or damaged components.

  1. Visually inspect the following items for wear:

    1. All recon and computer cables should be tight to the touch.

    1. Inspect the desk top, keyboard top, and their supports for damage and tightness.

    (Step Details)

1.5   System Power-Up

  1. Power-up console.

  1. Remove LOCK OUT /TAG OUT Device.

  1. Check that the mouse and keyboard cables are snug.

  1. Turn ON power panel breaker.

  1. Turn ON console front power switch.

    (Step Details)

  1. Check all console error logs for component problems.

1.6   Console Functional Check

  1. Check that all components operate normally. Replace any intermittent component.

  1. Replace the following items as needed:

    1. Keyboard

    1. Trackball

    1. SCIM

    1. Mouse

      (Step Details)

1.7   Monitor Adjustments - Optional (As Required)

  1. Set up the monitor contrast and brightness using the SMPTE Pattern.

    1. If you are not on the Service Desktop, click the [SERVICE DESKTOP] icon.

    1. Click the [IMAGE QUALITY] icon.

    1. Select [INSTALL SMPTE IMAGE]. (Approximately 3-4 minutes.)

  1. Display the SMPTE pattern. Use the browser to select Exam 1000, which contains the SMPTE pattern, and enlarge the image to full screen display.

  1. Increase the Contrast to maximum. Adjust monitor contrast until the anatomical structure (window width) appears at the expected window levels.

  1. Increase the Brightness to maximum. Adjust the monitor brightness the anatomical structure (window width) appears at the expected window levels.

  1. Decrease the Brightness, until the raster just fades into, and matches, the monitor screen background. At this point, the 5% and 95% patches should be just visible.

    (Step Details)

  1. To view SMPTE on text monitor, swap the video cable.
