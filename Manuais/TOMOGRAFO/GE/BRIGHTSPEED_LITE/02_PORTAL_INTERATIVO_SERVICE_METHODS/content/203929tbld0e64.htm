Table 1:  Actions Performed as ctuser

Type:Result
mount /dev/cdrom1This 'mounts' the DVD drive as a CD reader.
cd /mnt/cdrom1Shows all the files present on your CD (e.g., the PDF files)
acroreadThis opens the Acrobat Reader, where you find usual 'File Open' menus, etc... After the session, close Acrobat Reader.
cd ~Returns to ctuser's home.
umount /dev/cdrom1 
eject cdrom1Eject (or use push button)
