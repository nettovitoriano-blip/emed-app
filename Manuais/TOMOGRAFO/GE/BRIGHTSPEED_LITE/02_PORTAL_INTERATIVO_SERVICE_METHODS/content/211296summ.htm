Laser Light Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

NOTICE
Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.
 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

-

-

3  Procedure

  1. Remove gantry covers as required.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 

  1. Turn off the three (3) main power switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Disconnect the electrical connection to the defective laser assembly

  1. Loosen laser clamp nut.

  1. Attempt to remove the laser from clamp using the 5mm hex key. If additional clearance is needed, loosen 2 mounting screws NOT alignment screws (see Illustration 1). Remove mounting bracket if necessary.
