4  Procedure

  1. Remove right side cover.

NOTICE
Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 

  1. Turn OFF the three (3) power switches (Axial Drive, HVDC & 120VAC) on the gantry’s Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove the gantry top covers.

  1. Remove the connector and the screw that secures the temperature sensor board.

    Illustration 1:  Temperature Sensor Board

  1. Remove the old temperature sensor board.

  1. Install the new temperature sensor board.
