  Safety

WARNING
Impact Hazard.
Failure to Torque trim weight fastening hardware will result in damaging projectile events that will result in severe equipment damage and possible personal injury.
Torque all fasteners to proper levels.

NOTICE
Use the DAS Debris Shields to prevent metal shavings from contaminating the DAS Chassis. These shields can be used with the DAS fans running. Remove debris shields before rotating the gantry. Refer to the following illustration:

 

NOTICE
“Stacking Order” of balance weights is critical.

  • The order from back to front of the specific balance weights is shown in the figure below. Location angle 107 is shown, but also applies to the 180-degree location.

    Mandatory Weight Stack Positioning Order is shown below:

  • The balance program will determine the number of each specific weight.

  • The physical order from front to back is critical and cannot be altered.

  • A quantity of 0 (zero) is a valid number for specific weights.

  • Each specific weight is defined by its base metal, generic name, location angle and thickness in mm.

    Example:

    • Base Metal: Aluminum

    • Generic Name: SPACER

    • Location Angle: 107 DEG

    • Thickness in mm: 25.4MM

    NOTE:You may not have all plate types installed on your gantry depending on what is needed to balance your gantry.

1  Procedure

  1. Review the information in the Safety Section, above.

  1. Position the table to its lowest position.

  1. Remove the gantry side, top, front and rear covers.

  1. Launch the Service Gantry Balance program from the Service Desktop, Replacement Procedures tab.

    (Step Details)

  1. Select Run to start the gantry balance procedure.

    (Step Details)

  1. If the sensitivity matrix does not exist or the gantry is out of balance, the program will ask you to enter the current weight configuration prior to performing the steps to generate a new sensitivity matrix. See Illustration 4 for example of entries for 107° weight stack. The 180° weight stack appears in the same manner.

    (Step Details)

  1. Follow the steps as stated in the “Result” portion of the GUI screen as you proceed through the Gantry Balance procedure. Those steps will not be included here as they may change from release to release of the product.

    (Step Details)

  1. When the Gantry Balance procedure indicates a passing result save the updated configuration information and exit the program.

WARNING
Impact Hazard.
Failure to Torque trim weight fastening hardware will result in damaging projectile events that will result in severe equipment damage and possible personal injury.
Torque all fasteners to proper levels.
