Clean-up and Personal Hygiene

1  Slip Ring Brush Dust and Debris

After servicing slip ring components and surrounding areas, wash your hands thoroughly with soap and water. Dispose of personal protection equipment and rags properly.

Place all materials that contain brush debris (such as the HEPA vacuum cleaner filter, paper toweling, gloves, and old brush assemblies) in a sealed plastic bag and return to the GE Recycling Center:

GEMS Recycling Center
% ACE Warehouse
Building #11
2200 E. College Avenue
Cudahy, WI. 53110

2  Infection Control

When working on any Customer system in areas where possible bodily fluids may collect, be sure to follow all cautionary procedures as defined by Blood Borne Pathogens Hazards training. Some of these areas may be the Patient Table, Table Base, Cradle, Gantry Base, Lower covers, Trailer, Collimator and Scan Windows.
