DAS Converter Board Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

NOTICE
Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.
 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

-

-

3  Procedure

3.1   Board Removal

NOTICE
Follow ALL Electro-Static Discharge procedures. Always use ESD Wrist Strap and grounding cords. The use of a ground Monitor is suggested.
 

  1. Position the table to its lowest position.

  1. Remove gantry right side cover.

NOTICE
Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 

  1. Turn OFF Axial Enable, HVDC, and 120VAC switches on the Service Switch panel.

  1. Lock the gantry in position, using the rotational lock.

  1. Remove appropriate chassis cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Put on Protective Gloves.

  1. Slide Converter board out of chassis.

    1. Lift both red ejection tabs and the board will disengage from backplane connection.

      (Step Details)

    1. Continue sliding board straight out and place into anti-static bag.

3.2   Board Installation

NOTICE
Follow ALL Electro-Static Discharge procedures. Always use ESD Wrist Strap and grounding cords. The use of a ground Monitor is suggested.
 

  1. Remove New Converter Board from anti-static bag.

  1. Align Converter board edges to card guides in Chassis.

  1. Slide the board into the chassis until red ejector tabs align with the card guides (Illustration 3).

  1. Fold the Red tabs over to push board into place. Press in on tabs to fully seat board into the backplane connector - the board should move inward an additional millimeter (approximately).

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
Failure to fully seat a converter board into DAS backplane may result in the failure of all 128 channels in that converter board.
 

  1. Turn the Gantry 120V power ON, and verify no failures during power-up self-tests, via DCB LED status or System error log.

  1. Reinstall the Converter board chassis cover by first engaging all screws, and then tightening them.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disengage the rotational lock.

  1. Turn on Axial and HVDC switches.

4  Finalization

  1. Perform a DAS Reset from the Reset Menu.

  1. Depending on the fault, let the board warm-up five (5) minutes to verify it fixed the problem, but at least 1 hour before DASTools, FastCal, or Image Quality scans. A cold board may fail Offset Drift or Pop Noise until it is in normal operating temperature ranges.

  1. Verify proper functionality:

    1. Run at least 10 passes of Scan Data Path Diagnostic from Converter Boards.

    1. Run 1 pass of DASTOOLS.

    1. Run FastCal, if less than five (5) boards replaced.

    1. Run full FastCal and Phantom Cal, if more than five (5) boards replaced or if I/Q fails due to replaced boards.

      (Step Details)

    1. Take 10 I/Q scans of the 48cm phantom.

    1. Verify fault or reason to replace the board now passes.

  1. Reassemble the gantry covers.
