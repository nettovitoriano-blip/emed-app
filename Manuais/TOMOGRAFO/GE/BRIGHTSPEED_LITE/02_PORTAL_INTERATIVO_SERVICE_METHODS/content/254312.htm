Heater Diagnostic Test (LS16, Ultra, Plus)

(Click Here for Summary Version)

1  Personnel Requirements

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization
15 mins

2  Overview

Objective: To drive the heater inverter on both tube filaments in order to identify a faulty heater board or a wrong connection between the heater board, HV Tank and tube. Summary: Run diagnostic. The test is executed without high voltage or anode rotation, and needs no manual interaction.

Last Revised:June 2003

4  Procedure

  1. Launch the [GENERATOR TOOL].

  1. Launch the [HEATER DIAGNOSTIC] tool.

  1. Run the test. A scan sequence will proceed.

  1. If a problem occurs, check the error code.
