MANUAL SCAN PROTOCOL SETUP

Refer to Table 1 and setup an Axial scan with the parameters as shown.

AUTO SCAN PROTOCOL SETUP

  1. On the Exam Rx desktop, select [NEW PATIENT].

  2. Type the following entries in the two listed Patient Information fields:

    Patient ID: Service

    Name: 48cm Phantom Image Series

  3. From the Protocol display, click on the infant box.

  4. On the infant display window, click on the area below the infant’s right foot to display the Miscellaneous menu.

  5. Click on the [20:10 IMAGE SERIES 48 CM] button.

  6. Select the first series (Series Description filed should display 48cm Lg Series 1)
