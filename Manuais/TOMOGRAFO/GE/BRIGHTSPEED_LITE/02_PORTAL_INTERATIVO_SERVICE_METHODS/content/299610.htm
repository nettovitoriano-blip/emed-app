LCD Video Monitor Overview

Last Revised on:3 October 2008

This procedure covers the following topics:

  • Section 1 - General Monitor Information (cleaning, warm-up time, specifications, image persistence)

  • Section 2 - Monitor Controls and Informational Messages

1  General Monitor Information

1.1  Warm-Up Time

For optimum performance, allow 20 minutes for warm-up.

1.2  Environmental Considerations

1.2.1  Operating

  • Temperature: 5 - 35 °C (41 - 95 °F)

  • Humidity: 30 - 80%

  • Elevation: 0 - 10,000 feet

1.2.2  Storage

  • Temperature: -10 to +60 °C (14 - 140 °F)

  • Humidity: 10 - 85%

  • Elevation: 0 - 40,000 feet

1.3  Weight

9.7 kg (21.4 lbs)

1.4  Image Persistence

Image Persistence is when a residual or “ghost” image of a previous image remains visible on the screen. Unlike CRT monitors, LCD monitors’ image persistence is not permanent, but constant images being displayed for a long period of time should be avoided.

To alleviate image persistence, the Host Computer supplies an automatic Screen Saver. Also NEC monitors incorporates the use of the OFF TIMER feature to blank the screen after a period of none use. Do adjust the TIMER feature..

2  Monitor Controls and Informational Messages

NEC1990 SXi On Screen Manager (OSM) setup can be accessed by pressing Exit, Left, Right, Up or Down control buttons on the monitor. For a complete list of the OSM details go to On-Screen Manager Control Buttons
