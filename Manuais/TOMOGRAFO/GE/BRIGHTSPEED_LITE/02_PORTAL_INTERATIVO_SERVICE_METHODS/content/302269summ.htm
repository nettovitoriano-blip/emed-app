Gantry Display Assembly Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

CAUTION
Potential for Injury.
Close Working Space.
Position Front Cover Away from the Gantry to Allow Working Space Needed to Replace Display Assembly.

CAUTION
Potential for Injury.
A Ladder May be Required to Obtain Access to the Display Assembly.
Using the Table as a Lift is NOT a Safe Replacement for a Ladder.

NOTICE
Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.
 

NOTICE
Gantry damage will occur if cables are not properly secured to the gantry cover.
 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

-

-

3  Procedure

  1. Remove gantry covers as required.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 

  1. Turn off the three (3) main power switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove 4 cables.

  1. Loosen 6 screws that fasten the display assembly to the cover. Care should be taken to loosen all screws. Screws will break if over-tightened.

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace the display assembly panel and reconnect cables.

NOTICE
Always turn ON the 120 VAC before the HVDC. Turning ON HVDC power before 120 VAC power can result in equipment damage.
 

NOTICE
Gantry damage will occur if cables are not properly secured to the gantry cover.
 

  1. Reassemble gantry.

    (Step Details)

4  Finalization

  1. Power up system.

  1. Perform a flash down load for the display.

  1. Scan to confirm operation.
