Intercom Board Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Remove right side cover.

NOTICE
Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 

  1. Turn off the three (3) main power switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Use the quarter inch ratchet to loosen but not remove the 2 lower nuts and washers.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove 2 upper nuts and washers.

  1. Disconnect control cable.

  1. Remove 4 screws that secure intercom board.

  1. Reinstall intercom board in reverse order. Verify that J2 jumper is in auto position.
