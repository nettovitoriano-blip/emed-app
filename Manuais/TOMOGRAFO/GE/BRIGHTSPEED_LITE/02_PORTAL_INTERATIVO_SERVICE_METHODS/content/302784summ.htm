Gantry Tilt/Fan Relay (GTFR) Board Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

NOTICE
Wear a grounded wrist strap when you handle a circuit board.
 

NOTICE
Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.
 

NOTE:Some steps may require service in blind areas.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

-

-

Gantry tilt, if possible. May aid in this replacement process.

-

-

Gantry rotated to a position that offers the best service access, before turning off gantry 120/ 208 VAC.

-

-

3  Procedure

  1. Remove gantry covers as required.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 

  1. Turn off the three (3) main power switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Tilt gantry back 20 degrees.

NOTICE
Wear a grounded wrist strap when you handle a circuit board.
 

  1. Remove the three (3) nuts that fasten the plastic cover to the GTFR Board.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove J1 and the three *3( other connectors.

  1. Loosen 16 screws to release the GTFR board.

  1. Install new GTFR board and reassemble gantry.

4  Finalization

  1. Perform a Tilt Functional Test.
