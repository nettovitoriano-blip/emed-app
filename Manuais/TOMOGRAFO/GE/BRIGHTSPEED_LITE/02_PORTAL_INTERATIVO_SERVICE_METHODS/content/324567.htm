2  Overview

Fast Calibration ensures consistent image quality and radiation exposure by correcting for channel gain differences in the detector, detector drift correction resulting from radiation damage, imperfection in the bowtie filter, and removal of bowtie shape from the data.

Last Revised:October 22, 2009

3  Preliminary Requirements

3.1  Safety

CAUTION
Remove any objects from scan window area which may interfere with the rotating gantry.

CAUTION
No personnel or patients should be in the area of the gantry while scanning without adequate radiation safety precautions being utilized.
