21 SYSLITESteady-state +24Vdc input signal from the Gantry to close the “System ON” Light relay.
22 RDYLITESteady-state +24Vdc input signal from the Gantry to close the “Generator Ready” Light relay. NGPDU provides isolated terminals for customer Room Light connections.
23 XG_ENBL-CONTSteady-state +24Vdc input signal from the Gantry to indicate the HVDC Bus is enabled.
