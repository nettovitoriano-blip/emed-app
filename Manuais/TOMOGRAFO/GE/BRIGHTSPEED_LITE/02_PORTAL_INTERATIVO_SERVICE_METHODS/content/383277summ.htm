3  Procedure

3.1   Power-Off (Shut Down) the Console

  1. Select one of the following methods to Power OFF the Operator Console:

    1. If Applications are UP, click on the [Shut Down] button and select [Shutdown]. The Operator Console monitor will display a 'Power Down' message when it is acceptable to power OFF the Operator Console.

    1. If Applications are DOWN, open a Unix Shell. Type: halt.

  1. Power OFF the console at the front panel switch. (SeeIllustration 1.)

    (Step Details)

3.2   Remove the Rear Cover

Refer to Console Cover Removal and Installation, for details.

3.3   Replace the Existing Fan

  1. Disconnect the Power to the Fan. See Illustration 2.

    1. Firmly grasp the blue power Connector with one hand and firmly grasp the red power connector with the other hand.

    1. Pull apart.

    1. Repeat for the second power connector.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the four (4) screws that hold the Fan in place. Save the screws and the fan guard.

NOTE:Take Care to insure that the Air will be lowing out of the console, Use the arrow indicators on the Fan as a guide. The Arrow should be pointing out of the Console.

  1. Install the replacement fan using the four (4) screws, nuts and fan guard from the previous step.

  1. Reattach the power to the fan.

3.4   Test the Replacement Fan

  1. Apply power to the console.

  1. Verify that the fan is blowing air in the proper direction.

    (Step Details)

4  Finalization

  1. Install the console Rear cover.

  1. Turn the system ON.
