4  Procedure

  1. Remove side, top, and front gantry covers.

  1. Position the tube at 6 o'clock.

  1. Disconnect CAM encoder harness at the CCB chassis for both CAM motors and also disconnect CAM power harness at the CCB chassis for both CAM motors.

    Illustration 1:  Harness Connectors

  1. Cut the tie-wraps that secure the wiring harness of the CCB chassis (usually has 6).

    Illustration 2:  Tie-wraps

  1. Loosen the four (4) thumb-screws holding the front CCB chassis cover and remove the cover.

    Illustration 3:  Four CCB Cover Screws

  1. Disconnect connectors J3 and J4 filter motors and encoders from the CCB. Use access holes in chassis for back screws.

    Illustration 4:  J3 and J4 - disconnecting

  1. Position the CAM cables out from the CCB / Chassis assembly, and disconnect the power cable.

    Illustration 5:  Cam Cables

  1. Remove the ground screw and disconnect the grommet.

    Illustration 6:  Ground and Grommet

  1. Disconnect collimator connector J7.

    Illustration 7:  Collimator J7

  1. Remove cable clamp screws holding filter motor leads.

    Illustration 8:  Cable Clamp

  1. Remove the four (4) M6 screws holding the CCB/Chassis assembly to the collimator.

    Illustration 9:  CCB Chassis Mounting Screws

  1. To Install new CCB, Carefully route the wire harnesses through the CCB and position the CCB Chassis assembly.

  1. Install the four (4) mounting screws.

  1. Position the filter motor leads and install the cable clamp screws.

  1. Connect collimator connector J7 and torque to 3.0 Nm (26.6 lb-in).

  1. Position the grommet and ground wire, install the ground screw.

  1. Connect the power cable and reposition the cables inside the chassis.

  1. Connect J3 and J4 connectors and torque to 3.0 Nm (26.6 lb-in).

  1. Install the six (6) new tie-wraps securing the wiring harness.

  1. Connect the CAM encoder and power harnesses at the cam motors.

  1. Install the chassis cover.

  1. Install all the covers.

5  Finalization

  1. For new CCB, system should auto-detect new CCB to synchronize system proper characterization file / serial number. Follow on-screen instructions.

  1. For new CCB, it may be necessary to run Flash Download.

  1. For new CCB, perform:

    Detailed Calibration
