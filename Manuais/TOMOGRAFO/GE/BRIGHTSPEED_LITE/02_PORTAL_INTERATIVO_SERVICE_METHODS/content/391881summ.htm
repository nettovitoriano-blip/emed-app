GDAS Astec Analog PS Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

DANGER
Electrocution hazard
High Voltages present.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

DANGER
CRUSH HAZARD.
UNEXPECTED GANTRY ROTATION CAN CAUSE SERIOUS INJURY.
Never service the gantry with the axial drive enabled. Use the gantry rotation lock to lock out gantry rotation. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

CAUTION
Potential for injury to service personnel.
Each power supply chassis weighs just under 30 pounds.
Depending on your height, a step stool may be required to reach over the TGP for supply removal/installation. Use your judgment to determine if a second person may be required to assist in removal and installation.

CAUTION
Burn injury possible.
Power supply may be hot to touch
Use caution to prevent possible burn injury from hot surface contact.

NOTICE
Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.
 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

3  Procedure

3.1   Prepare the Table and Gantry

  1. Position the table so it is out of the way.

  1. Remove gantry side covers.

    (Step Details)

DANGER
Electrocution hazard
High Voltages present.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

  1. From the right side of the gantry, turn off the Axial Drive, HVDC, and 120VAC Switches.

  1. Remove the top covers.

    1. Unplug the top cover fans.

    1. Remove both top covers.

  1. Rotate the gantry to access the Astec analog power supply from the left side (same side as TGP).

  1. Use the Index Lock to lock the gantry.

    (Step Details)

3.2   Remove the Astec Power Supply

  1. If necessary, loosen the reusable Cable Clamps where found.

  1. Remove the connector restraint bracket over the J1 Connector.

  1. Disconnect cables to J1 and J4.

    (Step Details)

CAUTION
Burn injury possible.
Power supply may be hot to touch
Use caution to prevent possible burn injury from hot surface contact.

  1. Remove the four (4) bolts and lock washers as shown in Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the power supply assembly from the gantry.

    (Step Details)

3.3   Install the Replacement Power Supply

  1. Insert the new power supply. Take advantage of the guide pins on the rotating base to hold the new power supply in position.

NOTE:Do not use Loctite compound on these bolts.

  1. Use the new bolts and lock washers provided. Tighten all four (4) bolts to the specified torque setting shown in Table 1.

    (Step Details)

NOTE:The connector thumbscrews of the ASTEC Analog power supply and backplane connector harnesses WILL BREAK very easily if using a large diameter handle screwdriver. A gentle hand torque using a small diameter handle screwdriver is recommended.

  1. Reconnect J1 and J5.

  1. Reroute any cables back through the removable cable clamps and tighten them.

    (Step Details)

4  Finalization

NOTICE
Possible Equipment Damage.

Service Action required the removal of either rotating or stationary gantry sub-assemblies. Make sure that the gantry freely rotates, without interference.

 

  1. Remove the Index Lock and all safety equipment.

  1. MANUALLY rotate the gantry several times while inspecting the power supply to make sure that all cables and other hardware removed are properly secured to the rotating side of the gantry, and are not interfering with rotation.

  1. From the right side of the gantry, turn ON the Axial Drive, HVDC, and 120VAC switches.

  1. Replace the top covers and connect the top cover fans.

WARNING
Potential for electric shock.
Voltage is Present.
Failure to use an insulated voltage adjustment “Tweaker” can result in personal injury and/or component damage.

  1. Adjust 5V supply's output. Refer to GDAS Power Supply Adjustment procedure.

  1. Replace the side covers.

NOTE:Rebalancing the gantry is not required.

  1. Perform a basic scan to ensure the system is functioning normally.
