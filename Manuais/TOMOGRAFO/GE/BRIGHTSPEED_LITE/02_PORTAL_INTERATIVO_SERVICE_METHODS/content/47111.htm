4  Procedure

  1. Move table to its lowest elevation.

  1. Remove gantry covers as required.

    Refer to Parts Replacement → Gantry → Enclosure → (Cover Removal Procedure).

NOTICE
Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 

  1. Turn OFF all 3 service switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the Service Switch Panel.

DANGER
Electrocution hazard
High Voltage present.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

  1. Perform Lockout Tagout for system power (A1 disconnect) prior to replacing this component.

  1. Remove Rear Gantry cover support bracket.

  1. Remove Slipring covers.

  1. Disconnect all connections to Brush block (see Illustration 1).

    Illustration 1:  Slipring Brush Block Assembly

  1. Remove four (4) 6mm cap screws that secure brush block assembly to gantry.

  1. Carefully remove brush block.

  1. Inspect each brush tip for wear. Each tip will have a triangle stamped on one side. When the brush wears to the point of the triangle the brush must be replaced.

NOTICE
Brush Contamination Possible. Do not touch brushes with your fingers. The skin oils will contaminate the brush and reduce usable life and potentially create future failures.
 

  1. Remove individual brushes from the block as necessary by unscrewing brush cap and extracting brush.

    NOTE:Since brushes are spring-loaded to ensure constant contact with the slipring during operation, the springs will relax when the block is removed, causing brushes to bound outwards.

    If brush is to be reused make sure you install it in the same orientation as removed. The brush was seated/conditioned in that position.

NOTICE
Brush Tip Damage Possible. Brush tips are extremely brittle. Do not apply sideways force as they will break. Any brush that has been damaged in this fashion must be replaced.
 

  1. Carefully install brush block by exerting even pressure perpendicular to the ring surface.

  1. Secure brush block with the four (4) 6 mm cap screws. Do not tighten yet.

  1. Carefully push brush block against the position adjustment set screws in the mounting bracket.

  1. Remove two (2) brushes from the inside HVDC ring top and bottom. Remember the orientation for later installation.

  1. Use a flashlight to verify block position is adjusted so the brushes ride in the center of their tracks. Adjust the brush-block setscrews if necessary to center the brush.

  1. Torque four (4) 6 mm cap screws to 7.9 N-m (70 in-lbs or 5.8 ft-lbs).

  1. Reassemble gantry.

5  Finalization

  1. Hardware Reset using console gantry reset (Hardwire).

  1. Acquire 10 scouts: (120kV/40mA., 1000mm table movement)

  1. Acquire 100 axials: (120kV/80mA., 0.5 sec. Scan)

  1. Acquire 1 helical: (120kV/40mA., 30 sec. Scan)

  1. Acquire 10 axial scans: (120kV/400mA., 4 sec. Scan)

  1. Verify NO increase in LSCOM errors.
