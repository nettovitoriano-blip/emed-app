Axial Drive Holding Brake Replacement

1  Safety

DANGER
Electrocution hazard
High Voltages present.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

DANGER

UNEXPECTED GANTRY ROTATION CAN CAUSE SERIOUS INJURY.
Never service the gantry with the axial drive enabled. Use the gantry rotation lock to lock out gantry rotation.

Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.

3  Procedure

  1. Remove gantry covers as required.

    (Step Details)

DANGER
Electrocution hazard
High Voltages present.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

  1. Turn off all three (3) gantry service switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) and perform Lockout/Tagout procedures.

DANGER
CRUSH HAZARD.
UNEXPECTED GANTRY ROTATION CAN CAUSE SERIOUS INJURY.
Never service the gantry with the axial drive enabled. Use the gantry rotation lock to lock out gantry rotation.

  1. Engage indexer lock to prevent unexpected gantry rotation.

  1. Disconnect 2 MOLEX connections to holding brake relay.

    (Step Details)

NOTE:It may be necessary to remove the Axial drive module for easier access. If you need to remove the Axial drive module refer to Axial Drive Module replacement for instructions.

  1. Remove three (3) bolts that secure BRAKE motor to MOTOR.

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide assembly off shaft.

  1. Check the collar alignment on the NEW Holding Brake before installing on motor shaft. The hexagonal opening should be centered to insure there are no grinding noises from the brake when in operation.

  1. If the Hexagonal opening is not centered perform Step 9, Step 10, and Step 11.

DANGER
SEVERE INJURY OR DEATH CAN RESULT.
120 VAC IS PRESENT AT HOLDING BRAKE RELAY.
Disable Gantry 120 VAC prior to servicing Holding brake.

  1. Connect two (2) Molex connectors to new Holding Brake while it is not attached to the motor shaft. This will release the brake.

  1. Center the Hexagonal opening with respect to the circular shaft opening.

  1. Remove the two (2) Molex connections. The brake will engage when power is remove.

  1. Install new brake.

  1. Reassemble gantry.

4  Finalization

  1. Perform Axial Brake Check located in Axial Motion Checks.

  1. Exercise alignment lights, and acquire several 1 second scans.
