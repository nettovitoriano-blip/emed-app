Home Flag and Sensor Board Assembly Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

CAUTION
Potential for Injury.
A Ladder May be Required to Obtain Access to the Display Assembly.
Using the Table as a Lift is NOT a Safe Replacement for a Ladder.

NOTICE
Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization when working on this product.
 

NOTE:Some steps may require service in blind areas.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

3  Procedure

  1. Move table to its lowest elevation.

  1. Remove gantry covers as required.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 

  1. Turn OFF all three (3) service switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC).

  1. Locate the home flag by rotating gantry. Replace if any damage is visible.

  1. Locate the Home Flag Sensor assembly. (See Illustration 1.)

  1. Disconnect the harness.

  1. Remove the M6 screws attaching the bracket to the frame.

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace Sensor Assembly and Home Flag as necessary.

  1. Slowly rotate gantry by hand and adjust flag position to pass through the center of the opto-sensor on the sensor board. Make sure the flag is parallel to the opto-sensor.

  1. Perform “Resetting the C-Pulse” (located in Align, Setup, Cals → Gantry folder).

  1. Reassemble gantry.

4  Finalization

  1. Perform Axial Home Flag Check, located in Axial Motion Checks.

  1. Perform System Scanning Test (located in Functional Checks → System folder).
