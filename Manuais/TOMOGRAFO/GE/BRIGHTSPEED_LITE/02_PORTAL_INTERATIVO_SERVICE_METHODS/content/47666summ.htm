ConditionReferenceEffectivity

3  Procedure

  1. Remove gantry covers as required.

    (Step Details)

WARNING
Potential for Shock
Voltage may be present.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

NOTICE
Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 

  1. Turn OFF all three (3) gantry service switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC). Follow Lockout/Tagout procedures.

  1. Using the hex socket, remove two (2) cap screws from the back side of supply located on the rear right fan assembly.

  1. Remove safety plate.

  1. Disconnect wiring.

  1. Replace the slipring power supply assembly.

4  Finalization

  1. Perform System Scanning Test (located in Functional Checks → System folder).
