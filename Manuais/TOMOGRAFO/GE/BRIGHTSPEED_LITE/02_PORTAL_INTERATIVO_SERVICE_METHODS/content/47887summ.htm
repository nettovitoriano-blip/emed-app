Fuse Box Switch Assembly Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

DANGER
Electrocution hazard
High Voltages present.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

DANGER
CRUSH HAZARD.
UNEXPECTED GANTRY ROTATION CAN CAUSE SERIOUS INJURY.
Use the gantry rotation lock to lock out gantry rotation. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

NOTICE
Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization when working on this product.
 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

-

-

3  Procedure

  1. Remove gantry covers as required.

    (Step Details)

DANGER
Electrocution hazard
High Voltages present.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

  1. Turn off all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC). Remove all system power at the Main Disconnect panel and use proper Lockout/Tagout procedures.

  1. Rotate Fuse Box Assembly to 10 o’clock.

DANGER
CRUSH HAZARD.
UNEXPECTED GANTRY ROTATION CAN CAUSE SERIOUS INJURY.
Use the gantry rotation lock to lock out gantry rotation. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

  1. Lock the gantry in position, using the rotational lock.

  1. Remove the hex screws that secure Fuse Box Assembly.

  1. Slide fuse box up so that washers fit through aperture on fuse box, and then release the fuse box.

  1. Remove the DHCB (Detector Heater Control Board) mounted on the side of the fuse box.

  1. Cut any necessary tie-wraps.

  1. Disconnect all associated terminals and cables.

  1. Install new Fuse Box Assembly and reconnect terminals/cables.

  1. Reinstall the DHCB (Detector Heater Control Board).

  1. Reassemble gantry.
