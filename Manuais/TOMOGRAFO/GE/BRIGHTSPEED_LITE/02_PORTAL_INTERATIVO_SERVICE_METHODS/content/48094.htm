2  Overview

The System Scanning Test provide the necessary steps to exercise the system and to ensure system integrity before releasing.

3.2  Safety

CAUTION
No personnel or patients should be in the area of the gantry while scanning without adequate radiation safety precautions being utilized.
Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization when working on this product.

NOTICE
Avoid Detector Damage. Never scan above 50 mA without first placing a phantom in the field of view. Levels in excess of 50 mA can cause temporary radiation damage to the detector that lasts several hours. If you acquire image series cals with a radiation damaged detector, the cals may cause artifacts in subsequent image series scans.

4  Procedure

4.1  Phantom Setup

  1. Record phantom serial number on PM Report Form.

  1. Perform Center Phantom.

  1. Set Internal Landmark.

4.2  Acquire Scans

  1. Select [NEW PATIENT]

  1. Type the following entries in listed Patient Information fields:

    1. Patient ID: Service

    1. Patient Name: System Scanning Test

  1. Select Service from Protocol Selection, Anatomical Selector.

  1. Select [MANUFACTURING].

  1. Select [SYSTEM SCAN/CUST QUAL RELI].

    NOTE:Not all software releases have this protocol at the release of this procedure. If your system does not have this protocol, manually enter the scan information from Table 1. Then complete all the listed scans. Continue procedure at Image Review

  1. Modify Series 1 - Scout as follows:

    NOTE:All Scout protocols are modified.

    1. Click [START LOC].

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes. First Scout scans are performed.

  1. Press [MOVE TO SCAN] on SCIM when it flashes.

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes. Second Scout scans are performed.

  1. Click [NEXT SERIES].

  1. Delete Groups 3 and 4 of Series 2.

    1. Select Group 4 and click [DELETE SELECTED GROUP].

    1. Select Group 3, if not already selected, and click [DELETE SELECTED GROUP].

  1. Press [MOVE TO SCAN] and [PRESCRIBED TILT] on SCIM when they flash.

    NOTE:Hold [PRESCRIBED TILT] until tilt position is reached.

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes. Helical scans are performed.

  1. Click [END EXAM]

  1. If PPS Response window is displayed, click [COMPLETE].

4.3  Image Review

  1. Select ImageWorks desktop.

  1. Select the scanned examination from List/Select browser.

  1. Click [VIEWER] and visually review all images from for unusual artifacts (rings, streaks, shading, cupping, noise, or center artifacts).

    NOTE:If any artifacts are identified, perform appropriate corrective action to resolve problem and retest.

  1. Record results on PM Report Form and store with system history records.
