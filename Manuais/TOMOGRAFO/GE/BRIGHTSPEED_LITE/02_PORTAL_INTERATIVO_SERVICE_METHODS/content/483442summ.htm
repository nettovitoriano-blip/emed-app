3  Procedure

3.1   Prepare Gantry for Receiver Replacement

  1. Remove the gantry right side cover.

  1. Turn OFF all 3 service switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Shut down system software and completely shut down system power. Use LOTO to remove power from gantry.

  1. Remove Gantry left side, top and rear covers.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the top Slip Ring safety cover.

3.2   Replace the Receiver

  1. The receivers are located above the Slip Ring Brush Block. Remove the top slip ring cover to gain access to the receivers.

  1. Remove the power and fiber cables from the receivers.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the two (2) M6 hex head screws (5 mm hex wrench).

  1. Remove the receiver assembly and put the new assembly in place.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the two (2) M6 hex head screws (5 mm hex wrench), and two (2) cables but do not tighten screws.

  1. Locate the Receiver gap tool and place it between slip ring antenna and receivers. The tool defines the necessary gap between receivers and slip ring.

3.3   Power-Up

  1. Remove the A1 Lock-Out Tag-Out and power up the console.

  1. Turn on the Gantry 120 VAC service switch and slowly rotate the gantry by hand while watching the gap between the receivers and antenna. Make sure the antenna never touches the two receivers.

  1. Turn off the gantry 120 VAC service switch and install the top slip ring cover.

  1. Turn on the 120 VAC, HVDC and Axial drive service switches and enable Table drives.

  1. Perform a [System Scanning Test] from the [Functional Checks] menu of the service manual to ensure proper data transmission.

  1. Check the RTS Dip stats for the scan just run and make sure there are no errors shown. If errors are shown, recheck alignments and cable connections.

  1. Turn off the Axial Drive, HVDC, and 120VAC service switches.

  1. Install the gantry rear cover, top covers and left side cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn on the 120VAC, HVDC and Axial drive service switches and enable Table drives.

  1. Install the gantry right side cover.

  1. No further finalization required since checks have already been done above.
