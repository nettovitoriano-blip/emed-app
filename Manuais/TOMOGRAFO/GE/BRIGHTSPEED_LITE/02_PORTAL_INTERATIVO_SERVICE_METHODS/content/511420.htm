4  Procedure

  1. Move table to longitudinal home position.

  1. Attach phantom holder to gantry end of cradle.

  1. Mount phantom to phantom holder.

  1. Place bubble level on top of phantom, positioned along Z-axis. Adjust phantom holder back knob until phantom is level front to back.

    Illustration 1:  Phantom Back Adjustment Knob

  1. Turn on laser alignment lights.

  1. Move table into gantry until axial internal alignment lights are aligned with phantom's axial reference mark.

  1. Adjust phantom holder side knob until sagittal internal alignment lights are aligned with phantom’s sagittal reference mark.

  1. Elevate table until coronal internal alignment lights are aligned with phantom’s coronal reference mark.

  1. Click [SCANNER UTILITIES].

  1. Click [CENTER PHANTOM].

  1. Click [CONFIRM].

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes.

  1. Adjust phantom position if results are out of specification range.

    NOTE:Phantom center specification is ± 0.5mm.

  1. Repeat steps 11 - 13 until the phantom is centered.

  1. Click [DONE] in Phantom Centering window.

  1. Click [QUIT] in Scanner Utilities window.
