NOTE:The CT/PET console assigns the AW a Direct Connect “AW IP Address” (which you copied down in Step 10). This IP address must be used to configure the AW for Direct Connect. If the AW4.3 has already been configured, you will need to re-configure it with the assigned IP address. Configuration of the AW for Direct Connect can be found in the AW4.3 Service Manual (5136311-100), Installation Job Cards List-Job Card IST 001. AW 4.3 Service Manual (5136311-100) may be accessed on the AW 4.3 by selecting AdminàService Docs and selecting the AW 4.3 Service Manual link.
