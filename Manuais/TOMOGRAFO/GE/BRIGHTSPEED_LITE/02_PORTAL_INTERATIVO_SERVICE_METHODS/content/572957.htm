3  Procedure

  1. Remove Right and Left Gantry side covers.

NOTICE
Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120VAC. Turning OFF 120VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove the 12 screws in the TGPU/MSUB covers.

    NOTE:The rear cover may overlay some of the screws on the TGPU/MSUB cover. By tilting the gantry forward or backward, FE can access these screws.

    Turn on 120VAC switch on the Service Switch Panel, then tilt the gantry forward or backward to access the screws which are overlaid by the rear cover. Turn off 120VAC switch on the Service Switch Panel. Then remove the screws. Repeat these steps can remove all the screws that are overlaid by the rear cover.

  1. Remove the covers.

  1. Disconnect all cabling into the TGPU.

  1. Remove the 8 screws in the TGPU.

  1. Remove the defective TGPU.

  1. Install the new TGPU in the reverse order.

  1. Execute Flash download query and update. In case the TGPU has a firmware later than OC, it may fail. In this case, execute the following NOTE:

    NOTE:Before powering ON the 120VAC Gantry power, open a shell and type the following to perform the FLASH download procedure so that TGPU firmware is downloaded from the OC.

    cd/usr/g/fw<Enter>

    touch tgpFtp<Enter>
