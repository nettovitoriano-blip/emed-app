TGPU Power Supply Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Remove Right and Left Gantry side covers.

NOTICE
Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120VAC. Turning OFF 120VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the service control panel.

  1. Remove the 4 screws that mount the TGPU Power Supply to the mounting pan.

  1. Disconnect the cables that connect to the power supply.

  1. Remove the TGPU Power Supply. See Illustration 1

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the new Power Supply in the reverse order. Connect cables by referring to the instruction pasted on the side of the Power Supply.
