Tilt Interference and Limit Switches Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

NOTICE
Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization when working on this product.
 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

-

-

3  Procedure

  1. Remove gantry covers as required.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn OFF all three (3) gantry service switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC).

  1. Remove the bolts to remove the switch. See Illustration 1 showing the tilt switch.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect molex connector to replace harness.

  1. Cut ty-raps as necessary.

NOTICE
Always turn ON the 120 VAC before the HVDC. Turning ON HVDC power before 120 VAC power can result in equipment damage.
 

  1. Install new switch assembly and adjust to make sure the switch is disengaged in the cam cutout and engaged on the high edge of the cam. Gantry power will need to be restored to allow the gantry to tilt for this adjustment.

  1. Reassemble gantry.

4  Finalization

  1. Verify full range of tilt operation.
