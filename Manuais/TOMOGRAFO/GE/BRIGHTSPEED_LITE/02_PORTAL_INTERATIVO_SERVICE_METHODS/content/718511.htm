4  Procedure

NOTICE
Understand and follow the Safety Guidelines Manual.
 

  1. Move the cradle OUT to its fully retracted position (farthest from Gantry).

  1. Raise the Table to its highest position.

  1. Remove power from Table by turning off “120VAC”, “Axial Drive” and “HVDC” switches on the service switch panel.

  1. Remove the six Allen screws from the cradle end, and then slide out the cradle.

    Illustration 1:  Cradle Replacement

  1. Install the new cradle.
