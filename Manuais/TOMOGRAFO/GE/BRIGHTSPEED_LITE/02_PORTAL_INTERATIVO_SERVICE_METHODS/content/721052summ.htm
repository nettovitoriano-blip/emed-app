Height Potentiometer Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

NOTICE
Understand and follow the Safety Guidelines Manual.
 

NOTE:It may be necessary to lower the Table to gain access to one of the height potentiometer coupling screws. If this is done then be sure to return the Table to its highest position, and return the Gantry “Table/Tilt” switch to the OFF position.

  1. Remove the left side maintenance cover from the Gantry.

  1. Set the service switch (MODE-SEL) to the SERVICE to enter the service mode.

  1. Confirm that the ”ELEV” LED on the GTCB board in the Table is ON.

    (Step Details)

  1. Upper the Table to the mechanical up limit position by the service switch (ACTION).

  1. Remove power from Table by turning off “120VAC”, “Axial Drive” and “HVDC” switches on the service switch panel.

  1. Remove the following covers and component from the Table:

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace the Height Potentiometer as follows:

    1. Loosen the two coupling set screws.

    1. Remove the Potentiometer Assy together with its bracket by unscrewing the two support nuts.

    1. Disconnect the connector J11 from the NPCN BD Assy and feed it up through the Table.

    1. Remove the Height Potentiometer assy from the bracket, and mount the new Potentiometer Assy onto the old bracket.

    1. Install the new Height Potentiometer/bracket Assy to the Table left side frame.

    1. Feed the wiring of the new Potentiometer to the NPCN BD Assy and retie all the tie-wraps which were removed in Step 7.c. and Step 7.d.

    1. Connect the new J11 connector to the NPCN BD Assy.

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform Elevation Characterization.

    (Step Details)

  1. Restore the Table to original configuration.
