1  Procedure

NOTICE
Understand and follow the Safety Guidelines Manual.
 

  1. Move the cradle to the OUT mechanical limit position by hand.

  1. Raise the Table to its highest position.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove power from Table by turning off “HVDC”, “120VAC” and “Axial Drive” switches on the service switch panel.

  1. Remove the following cover:

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the nine cable connectors (J1∼ J4, J6, J7, J11) from the NPCN Board Assy.

  1. Remove the NPCN Board Assy by unscrewing its four nuts.

  1. Install the new NPCN Board Assy by referring to Step 6 through Step 5.

  1. Restore the Table to original configuration.
