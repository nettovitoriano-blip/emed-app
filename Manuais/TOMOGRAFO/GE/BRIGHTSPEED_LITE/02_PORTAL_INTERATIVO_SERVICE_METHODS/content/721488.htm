4  Procedure

NOTICE
Understand and follow the Safety Guidelines Manual.
 

  1. Move the cradle to the OUT mechanical limit position by hand.

  1. Raise the Table to its highest position.

    NOTE:If the Table up/down movement is inoperative, use the service power cable to raise the Table (refer to Enforced Table Elevation).

  1. "Remove power from Table by turning off “120VAC”, “Axial Drive” and “HVDC” switches on the service switch panel."

  1. Remove the following cover from the Table:

    • Top Cover R / L (Refer to Table Covers Removal)

    • -

    Illustration 1:  Touch SW1 / SW2 Assembly Location

  1. Remove the Touch SW1/SW2 Assy from the Table top cover.

  1. Thread the Touch SW1/SW2 cable connector through the cable hole in the Table top cover, and attach the new Touch SW1/SW2 Assy to the Table top cover, then connect the Touch SW1/SW2 cable connector.

  1. Restore the Table to original configuration.

5  Finalization

  1. Verify that Touch SW1/SW2 Assy is operating normally.
