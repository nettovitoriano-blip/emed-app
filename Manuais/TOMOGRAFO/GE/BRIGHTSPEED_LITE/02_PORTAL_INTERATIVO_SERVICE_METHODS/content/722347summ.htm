Pump Assembly Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

NOTICE
Understand and follow the Safety Guidelines Manual.
 

  1. Remove the left side maintenance cover from the Gantry.

  1. Raise the Table to its highest position.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the following covers and component:

    (Step Details)

  1. "Remove power from Table by turning off “120VAC”, “Axial Drive” and “HVDC” switches on the service switch panel."

  1. Disconnect the power connector from the oil pump.

  1. Disconnect the 4-line connector leading from the NPSUB to the pump capacitor.

  1. Measure the resistance across the terminals of the pump capacitor using a DVM. If the resistance increases, the capacitor, the capacitor should be functional. If the resistance is zero or infinite, the capacitor is melted down or shorted open. In this case replace the Pump Capacitor.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the GND cable from the oil pump.

  1. Disconnect hose nuts A, B, and C shown in Illustration 2. A small amount of oil may leak out, but this is acceptable.

  1. Transfer the hose joints from the old pump to the new one.

  1. Remove the pump assy by unscrewing the two screws holding it to the Table base.

  1. Remove the pump base by unscrewing the four nuts, and transfer the pump base to the new pump.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the new oil pump assy by referring to Step 9 through Step 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. Add oil to the pump by referring to the following section:

    (Step Details)

  1. Lower the Table to its lower limit position.

  1. Raise the Table to its highest position.

  1. Repeat Step 15. and Step 16. a few times to remove any air in the cylinder. Stop this procedure at the Table upper limit position.

  1. Restore the Table to original configuration.
