AUTO SCAN PROTOCOL SETUP

  1. On the Exam Rx desktop, select [NEW PATIENT].

  2. Type the following entries in the listed Patient Information following fields:

    • Patient ID:Service

    • Name:20cm QA#3 Phantom Image Series

  3. From the Protocol display, click on the infant box.

  4. On the Infant display window, click on the area below the infant’s right foot to display the Miscellaneous menu.

  5. Click on the [20:12 IMAGE SERIES QA] button.

  6. When the scan prescription appears on the left monitor, select the 1st series.
