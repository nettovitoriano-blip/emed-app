Hydraulic Tilt Motor Speed Adjustment

3  Procedure

NOTE:Use of a laptop and the Common Service Desktop automated service tool is the preferred method for adjusting Hydraulic Tilt Motor speed for systems with 06MW29.7 or later software. If you do not have a laptop or 06MW29.7 (or later) software, there are two optional methods:

(For 2 users, NO laptop, 06MW29.7 or later software) Start the Tilt Speed tool at the Console from the Common Service Desktop. Have one person view the tool output at the console while the second person adjusts Tilt speed for one (1) degree per second motion.

(For 1 user or pre-06MW29.7 software) Use a stopwatch or suitable time piece to adjust Tilt speed for one (1) degree per second motion.

  1. (For users with laptop and 06MW29.7 or later software) Perform the following:

    1. Connect laptop to console using the service cable.

    1. Complete laptop setup and log in.

  1. (For users with 06MW29.7 or later software) Perform the following:

    1. Display the service desktop Diagnostics screen (from laptop or from the Console if no laptop).

    1. Select Left Side Troubleshooting Tool.

    1. Select Tilt Speed.

  1. Remove gantry rear base covers.

  1. From behind the gantry, use laptop to tilt gantry and adjust for 1° per second motion. Tilt speed will be displayed on the service tool.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
Adjustments are very sensitive. Improper speed adjustments will result in overspeed error generation.
 

NOTE:Tilt speed will vary based upon hydraulic fluid temperature. Adjustments should be made at normal scan room temperature settings. Gantry angle is also displayed. When you are at zero (0), the service tool display should read 0.00.

  1. From zero (0) degrees tilt, press and hold the tilt forward button, until gantry display reads S25. Observe tilt speed.

  1. Press and hold backward tilt button, until gantry display reads I25. Observe tilt speed.

  1. Adjust forward speed control valve for one (1) degree per second motion. See Illustration 1.

  1. Adjust backward speed control valve for one (1) degree per second motion. See Illustration 1.

  1. Repeat steps 3 through 6 until tilt speed for both directions is correct.

  1. Now tilt the gantry forward to S30. Observe the S25 to S30 speed. Do this several times. If a noticeable difference is observed, then check the hydraulic fluid levels.

    (Step Details)

4  Finalization

  1. Verify full range of tilt operation.
