1  Procedure

1.1   For Tube Change Only

Wait 90 minutes if the new tube had more than 25 Kilo Joules of energy input, [KV x mA x Sec ÷ 1000] within the last 30 minutes prior to the start of system alignments. If a tube heat soak has been performed you must wait a minimum of 6 hours before system alignments can be performed.

1.2   Confirm Cold Tube

When performing alignments, wait at least 15-30 minutes between scans to prevent unnecessary adjustments.

1.3   ISO Adjustment Procedure

  1. You should be familiar with POR Alignment before proceeding with ISO alignment.

  1. Select [Service] -> [Calibration], Run the [ISO ALIGNMENT]tool.

  1. Execute Air scan (small spot).

  1. Execute Air scan (large spot).

  1. Place the 1/8 inch screw driver on the phantom holder (should be pointing into the Z direction).

  1. Execute pin scan (small spot).

  1. Execute pin scan (large spot).

  1. <CALCULATE> ISO center alignment.

  1. Mount both dial indicators on the tube assembly - one for ISO alignment and one for POR. (Illustration 1.) Make sure that you zero both of them (POR and ISO).

    (Step Details)

  1. Loosen the 4 M-12 bolts on the tube assembly about 1/8 turn.

    (Step Details)

  1. Adjust the tube UP / DOWN as indicated by the calculation. The adjustment bolt for ISO is located on the top of the tube - Please see Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Tighten the four (4) M12 bolts and verify dial gauge still reads the correct adjustment value.

  1. Adjust M12 mounting bolts to pre-load torque specification. Refer to Table 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Apply final torque on all four M12 bolts according to the values specified in Table 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Repeat steps 1 through 9.

  1. If the adjustments are within limit proceed to the next step, otherwise go to step 10.

  1. Wait 15 minutes, then recheck BOW to verify values are within specification -1.5mm ±0.5mm.
