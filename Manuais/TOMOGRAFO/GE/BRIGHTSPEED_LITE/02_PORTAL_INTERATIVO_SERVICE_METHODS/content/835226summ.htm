3  Procedure

3.1   Sonic Meter Preparation

  1. Turn the Tension meter on.

  1. For the Gates Sonic Tension Meter model 507 C, press the Measure button and then press the Hz button. The green LED will be flashing. For an equivalent meter, make sure it is set p to measure frequency.

3.2   Sonic meter Setup Test

NOTE:The gantry belt tension has to be done at 4 different locations, as this is the most accurate (i.e., Tube at 0, 90, 180, 270 degrees).

  1. Position the Tube at 0 degrees while rotating by hand in the CW direction.

    (Step Details)

  1. From the Slip Ring side of the gantry hold the tension meter sensor half way between the motor pulley and idler pulley (upper side of belt) approximately 1/2 of an inch (1.5cm) from the top of the belt.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using your finger, pluck the belt or tap the belt lightly between the motor pulley and the idler pulley to make it vibrate. A waveform graphic will show up on the LCD screen. After the signal is processed properly, the belt frequency will appear on the screen and a successful measurement on the Gates meter will beep 3 times, and the green LED turns on steady to indicate a successful reading.

    (Step Details)

3.3   Adjusting Belt Tension

  1. Rotate gantry clockwise 2 turns to even out the belt tension.

  1. Rotate the gantry so the tube is at the 12 O'clock (0 degree) position.

  1. To unlock the tensioner assembly, loosen the two M12 screws one turn using a 10mm hex key. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. From the Slip Ring side of the gantry hold the tension meter sensor half way between the motor pulley and idler pulley (upper side of belt) approximately 1/2 of an inch (1.5cm) from the top of the belt.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using your finger, pluck the belt or tap the belt lightly between the motor pulley and the idler pulley to make it vibrate. A waveform graphic will show up on the LCD screen. After the signal is processed properly, the belt frequency will appear on the screen and a successful measurement on the Gates meter will beep 3 times, and the green LED turns on steady to indicate a successful reading.

  1. Write down the value acquired, (You will be averaging 4 readings at 0, 90, 180 and 270 degree gantry positions).

NOTE:A passing reading is in the range of 85 to 102.

  1. To adjust the belt, tighten the elongated hex screw using a 6mm hex key and a 12 inch ( 30.5cm) extension. Turn wrench in 1/4 turn increments. (Clockwise tightens the belt, counter-clockwise loosens the belt.) and repeat step 5 until you achieve a value in the range defined. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Tighten the two M12 screws to 66 N*m (50 FT*lb) to lock the tensioner assembly.

  1. Rotate the gantry to the next position (90 degrees).

  1. Once again, loosen the two M12 screws to unlock the tensioner assembly.

  1. Repeat the process as described in steps 5 through 7 for the 90, 180 and 270 degree locations achieving an average reading at all 4 locations between 85 and 120.

WARNING
Equipment damage
Belts should never be tensioned above a reading of 102.
It is not recommended to operate long term above 102.

  1. When you have a good average reading at all 4 positions, Tighten the two M12 screws to 66 N*m (50 FT*lb) to lock the tensioner assembly.

4  Finalization

  1. Run the gantry with motor power at .35 seconds for a couple of minutes

  1. Repeat steps 5 to ensure that ther belt is still within the 85 to 102 range, if not repeat this adjustment procedure until the belt frequency is within this range.
