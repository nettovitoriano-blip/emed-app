Verify Disk Array Drives

Open a Unix Shell and type the following, to verify Disk Array drives are present:

{ctuser@hostname}  rsh  darc

Last login: Tue Oct 7 14:58:34 from OC

{ctuser@darc}  cat  /proc/scsi/scsi/

NOTE:The output (shown below) may differ, due to various manufacturers of approved production released SCSI disk drives. However, two (2) 36 GByte drives must be present for a good Disk Array.

An example output is shown below.
